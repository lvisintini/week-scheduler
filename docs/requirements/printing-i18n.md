# Printing, agendas, translations

- **R-PRN-01** **[U]** **Day agenda** (click a day header): items shown that day in time order (time, title, person, with, schedules, ⚠, description; continuation pieces say "from Mon 22:00"), printer-friendly, with the visible schedules and people listed at the top (and the week, with a cycle of several weeks, R-CYC-12); with a rhythm shown, grouped under its phases (R-PRN-04).
- **R-PRN-02** **[U]** **Print schedule**: current grid on A4 portrait (the day view while focused on one day, R-GRID-11) in light colours, with the visible schedules and people listed at the top. With a cycle of several weeks Print offers *This week* / *Every week* (one page per week, each titled with its week, R-CYC-12). Mechanism: hidden `#pr` is filled, `window.print()`, `@media print` hides everything else.
- **R-PRN-03** **[U]** **Print day view**: one-column clone of the grid for that day only (all boxes and phases as on screen; lips regenerated).
- **R-PRN-04** **[U]** **Phases as headings in the day agenda** (chosen by the user over one line of phase chips at the top **[U]**): while a rhythm is shown (R-PHS-02), the phases of that day (and of the shown week, R-CYC-16) are **headings in start order** — a band in the phase's colour (a 22 % tint with a solid left edge), the name and the times, the description as a tooltip — and **each event is listed under the phase its piece starts in**; overlapping phases each list an event that starts in both; a phase with no events says *(nothing)*. Events that start outside every phase go under **Outside the rhythm**, one such group per gap, at its place in time. Without a rhythm shown the agenda is the plain time-ordered list. The printed agenda (*Print agenda*) keeps the headings, colours printed exactly. Times are in the normal text colour (grey on the tint was below 4.5:1).
- **R-I18N-01** **[U]** English/Spanish. Language from `navigator.languages[0]` (`es*` → Spanish), switchable in Settings, not persisted. Dynamic strings: add a key to both `D.en` and `D.es`, use `L('key',{var})`; static dialog HTML: English in the HTML plus an `['English prefix','Spanish']` entry in `ES` (keys <12 chars match exactly; longer by prefix; `applyStatic()` handles `<dialog>` only). Always `esc()` user text in `innerHTML`.
- **R-I18N-02** **[U]** **Household/workplace agnostic wording:** descriptions, tooltips and help never assume a home or a workplace. Where the place has to be named they say **household/workplace** (*hogar o lugar de trabajo*), and examples mix both (the cleaner at work, quiet hours, screen-free time; a cleaner, a babysitter, a contractor; picking up the post on the way back from lunch; a business trip, a training course). Short labels stay short: *In the background (doesn't keep us busy)* (*En segundo plano (no nos ocupa)*), *External (not one of us)* (*Externa (no es de los nuestros)*). Away is *Ausente* in Spanish (not *Fuera de casa*) **[U]**. Names are not covered: the default activity types (*Work/School*, *Chore*/*Tarea doméstica*) and the sample data keep their wording. A test scans every English and Spanish text and `title` for household-only phrases.
