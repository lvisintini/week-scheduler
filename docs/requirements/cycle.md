# Cycles of several weeks

- **R-CYC-01** **[U]** **A cycle of 1–4 weeks:** a workspace repeats every 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks (`cycle`, default 1, shared by all its schedules; stored only when above 1). Weeks are **numbered 1–4** (*Semana 1*) **[U]**, positions in the cycle, never calendar or ISO weeks: **the app holds no dates** **[U]**, with **two exceptions** **[U]**, both remembered only in this browser: the date that tells it which week today is in (R-CYC-05), and the period of the last calendar export (R-SHARE-09). The grid still shows **one week** at a time (the week switcher, R-CYC-04). Besides the cycle, a workspace may keep **staged weeks** after it (R-CYC-21). Typical uses: a two-week rota, fortnightly activities, different days in alternate weeks. Set with **Weeks in the cycle…** in the workspace menu (R-DATA-01) **[C✓]** — it is workspace data (undoable, an unsaved change), not a per-browser Setting and not a Tools view; its dialog has a 1–4 list and explains patterns and *Different per week* in its tooltip. **Out of scope [U]:** month-based rules (*the first Saturday of the month*: months are not 4 weeks) and dated exceptions (holidays, a skipped week).
- **R-CYC-02** **[U]** **Week pattern of an event** (the normal case): `event.weeks` = the weeks in which **every** repetition of the event happens, e.g. `[1,3]`; **absent = every week** **[U]** (every kept week, staged weeks included, R-CYC-21) — what all existing data, every new event and an event at cycle 1 mean, so lengthening the cycle never turns a weekly routine into a fortnightly one, and lunch stays 7 repetitions in a 4-week cycle instead of 28 **[U]**. Stored sorted and unique; a pattern naming every kept week is stored as absent **[C✓]**. Repetitions of a patterned event carry no `wk`. The peek card, agenda and `aria-label` name the weeks of an event that is not every week (*Weeks 1 and 3*).
- **R-CYC-03** **[U]** **Weeks per repetition** (irregular patterns, verbose by design **[U]**): every repetition carries `slot.wk` (1…the weeks kept, `pool`, R-CYC-21) and the event has no `weeks` — for Mon/Wed in week 1 and Tue/Thu in week 2, a stable per-week performer (a stand-in on that week's line) or a different time in week 3. **The mode is inferred, never flagged** **[C✓]**: an event is per-repetition when its repetitions carry `wk`. `parseData` normalises: in a per-repetition event a repetition without `wk` gets week 1 and `weeks` is dropped; pattern weeks and repetitions whose week is above the weeks kept (`pool`) are dropped (an event left without repetitions or with an empty pattern is dropped, like any event without repetitions) **[C✓]**.
- **R-CYC-04** **[U]** **Week switcher:** chooses the week the grid shows. **View state** like *Focus on one day* (R-GRID-11): not undoable, not an unsaved change, not saved, kept by undo/redo, clamped when the cycle gets shorter; the page starts on **today's week** when this browser knows it, else on week 1 (R-CYC-05); switching to another workspace shows that workspace's today's week when known. **[C✓]** It is a button in the grid's **top-left corner cell** (`.dh.c`, sticky both ways, so it stays in view while scrolling, in the day view and on a phone), showing *W2* / *S2* (`aria-label` *Week 2 of 3, change the week*), and opens a menu listing the weeks (the shown one ticked; whenever today's week is known it is marked *Week 2 · today*, R-CYC-05) and, except while live, *All weeks side by side* (R-CYC-19) and *Weeks in the cycle…*. Hidden at cycle 1. **Rejected:** a sidebar control (invisible on a phone and while the sidebar is collapsed). **Focus ◀ ▶ and the phone swipe step through the cycle** **[C✓]**: Sunday ▶ = Monday of the next week (the last week wraps to week 1), ◀ the reverse; the day-view header adds the week (*Tuesday · W2*). Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-05** **[U]** **Today's week (Live View's anchor):** one of the two dates the app keeps, an exception to *no dates* (R-CYC-01) chosen by the user over asking again after every reload **[U]**. Starting Live View (R-GRID-12) in a workspace with a cycle longer than one week whose week this browser does not know yet **asks which week of the cycle today is in** (a small dialog, the shown week preselected; *Cancel* does not start Live View and remembers nothing). The answer is kept as an **anchor: the Monday of a week 1** (local date `YYYY-MM-DD`), **remembered in this browser per workspace** (`prefs.lvAnchor`, keyed by workspace id, R-DATA-06) **[U]** — never in the workspace data, browser memory of the workspaces, files or links; not undoable and not an unsaved change. **Today's week** = the whole weeks from the anchor to this week's Monday, counted in local calendar days (clock changes cannot move it) and wrapped by the cycle: so **at midnight Live View moves to the next day, and after Sunday to Monday of week N+1, wrapping from the last week to week 1** **[U]**, and a tablet that slept for days still lands on the right week **[C✓]**. **After a reload** a remembered Live View starts at once on today's week, without asking **[U]**; a workspace whose week is not known yet asks (Cancel turns the remembered Live View off). **Correcting it:** the corner switcher stays pressable in Live View **[U]** (the one exception to the read-only grid): choosing another week jumps to today's day and now point in that week **and re-anchors** (remembered); outside Live View the switcher only changes the week shown. **Per workspace [C✓]:** switching to a workspace while live shows its own today's week, or asks for it (*Show this week* / *Cancel*, which stops Live View); a workspace that gets a cycle while live asks at the next 30-second tick; *Duplicate* copies the anchor, *Delete* and *Clear memory* forget it; reordering the weeks moves it with today's week (R-CYC-20); the **calendar export** asks for it in the same way when it is not known and works out the week of any date from it (`wkAt`, R-SHARE-09), so the answer serves both; a cycle of one week stores nothing; changing the cycle length keeps the anchor (the week is worked out again with the new length; *keep in step*, R-CYC-10, keeps every-other-week events in line). **Outside Live View [U]:** the page opens on today's week when known (R-CYC-04), a phone opens on today's day view in that week (R-UI-13), the week menu marks it *· today*, the corner button's tooltip says *Today is in week N (remembered in this browser; to correct it, change the week while Live View runs)*, and the now line is drawn only in today's week (R-GRID-04); while it is not known the switcher starts on week 1 and the now line marks today's weekday in whatever week is shown. The date itself is never shown. Loading `prefs` keeps only well-formed entries (id up to 64 characters, `YYYY-MM-DD`, at most 200) in an object without a prototype, so no workspace id can reach `Object.prototype`.
- **R-CYC-06** **[U]** **Week wrap:** the cycle is one timeline of n × 7 days. A repetition past midnight on **Sunday of week k runs into Monday of week k+1; the last week runs into week 1** (at cycle 1 this is today's Sunday → Monday wrap, R-REP-03). A patterned event (R-CYC-02) whose Sunday falls in week 1 only shows its continuation on Monday of week 2. Pieces, overlaps (R-REP-05), drags (R-CYC-09) and statistics (R-CYC-11) all use it.
- **R-CYC-07** **[U]** **Per-week rules:** occurrences in different weeks never meet. **Clashes, lanes, stripes and the ⚠ of overlapping repetitions** are computed from the occurrences of one week **[U]**; so are nesting (host and nested in the same week), background strips and their gutter, hit-testing, choosers, the peek card, Live View bubbles and *N hidden* **[C✓]** — the grid draws and finds only the shown week (with every week side by side, each column its own week, R-CYC-19). The ⚠ of a repetition means it clashes in some week of the cycle. **Fixed lanes** (R-EVT-23): the check that gives an external person a lane is **cycle-wide**, so lanes do not jump when switching weeks **[C✓]**.
- **R-CYC-08** **[U]** **Dialog:** while the cycle is longer than one week, *When* has a **Weeks** row above the day table: toggle chips **1 … n** (all ticked = every week, stored as absent; at least one) and a **Different per week** switch (tooltips explain both, and that complex rotas are better kept as owner + involved people, R-CYC-15). **Pattern mode** keeps today's Mon–Sun table unchanged. **Different per week** hides the chips and adds a **Wk** column (a list 1…n, *Week*) to every time of the day table (R-REP-02), chosen by the user from three mock-ups **[U]**: lines are kept in day, week, start order; a day is ticked when it has times in any week; ⧉ copies all of a day's times **with their weeks**; switching it on copies each time into every week of the pattern. **[C✓]** A day's **+** proposes the same times in the next week that has none on that day yet (otherwise R-REP-02's rule: after the day's last time); a newly ticked day starts in the week shown on the grid; each time's week is chosen from its button's menu (*Monday: week 2*). **[C✓]** **Switching back to a pattern:** when every day's times are the same in each week that has any, the pattern becomes those weeks; otherwise a confirmation names the week whose times are kept (the first that has any) and says the other weeks' differences are dropped. Opening from a week-2 occurrence pulses its own week-2 line (R-UI-12). Under 560 px *Wk* joins the first line of each time's small grid. **Rejected:** a table section per week (28 day rows at 4 weeks) and week tabs (they hide the other weeks) **[U]**. The week of each time has a **column of its own, headed *Week***, after *Days* on a computer, and on a phone card it heads the title (*Week [1▾] – Monday*, R-UI-07) **[U]**. Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-09** **[C✓]** **Dragging through the cycle:** a **patterned** event drags exactly as today (moves shift days mod 7 and the pattern is untouched: the weeks belong to the event, not to a repetition; changing one week means switching the event to *Different per week*). A **per-repetition** event's "all repetitions" move is a **true shift along the cycle** (Sunday of week 1 + 1 day = Monday of week 2; the last week wraps to week 1); Shift changes only the grabbed repetition, which is one occurrence; *Only this day* puts it back in its week; the "at most one time per day" check of R-REP-06 counts per (week, day); the nested carry (R-NST-03) follows its host's shift, week included. Moving a time to another week is done in the dialog (its *Wk*) or across Sunday midnight. Times of staged weeks never move with them (R-CYC-22).
- **R-CYC-10** **[U]** **Changing the cycle length** (one undo step, nothing removed silently): **lengthening** keeps every-week events every week (only the Sunday overflow moves on to the next week) **[U]**; going to a **multiple** (2 → 4) **offers to extend patterns and per-week events periodically** (ticked by default: `[1]` → `[1,3]`, week-2 times copied to week 4) so "every other week" keeps its meaning **[U]**; going to a non-multiple (2 → 3) keeps the week numbers and the dialog says that "every other week" cannot be kept in that cycle **[C✓]**. **Shrinking** **[C✓]** lists the events that lose weeks (and those that would lose every occurrence) and offers: **keep them as staged weeks** (the default, R-CYC-21) **[U]**, **remove those occurrences**, or **move them into a new schedule per removed week** (named *Week 3*, holding them as week-1 repetitions of copies), then removes what is left over; removing and moving also drop those weeks (later staged weeks move down); *Cancel* changes nothing. The question is asked whenever the weeks that leave hold something (events or phases chosen for them, or a name, description or colour); weeks that hold nothing are simply dropped. **Lengthening when staged weeks exist [C✓]** brings the staged weeks back into the cycle as they are; only weeks beyond the kept ones are new, and *keep chosen weeks in step* is offered only when every new week is new (no staged weeks). The shown week is clamped. Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-11** **[U]** **Statistics per cycle:** totals over the whole cycle plus **an average per week**, so fairness is visible **[U]**. **[C✓]** The statistics dialog (R-STAT-01) gets a week list, **Average week** (default) | *Week 1* …: the 168-hour bar, day bars and table show that week or the average; the table adds a column per week, *Average* and *Cycle total*; *Everyone* shows each person's planned hours per week; the CSV and *Copy table* get the same columns. For the average week the overlapping line counts the whole cycle (*Overlapping over the whole cycle: h:mm in N place(s)*). Hidden at cycle 1, where everything is as before. Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-12** **[U]** **Print, agendas and *N hidden* follow the shown week**, with an option to **print every week** **[U]**: with a cycle longer than one week, Print opens a two-item menu, *Print this week (week 2)* / *Print every week (one page each)*; in the day view, every week prints that day of each week **[C✓]**; printed pages, the day agenda and its title name the week (*Week 2*) **[C✓]**; *N hidden* counts only the shown week's occurrences (every week while they are side by side, R-CYC-19). Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-13** **[C✓]** **Insert and transfers between cycles** (R-DATA-03/04): importing as a new workspace or replacing needs nothing (the record brings its own cycle). **Insert into the current workspace** and **copying/moving a schedule** keep week numbers as they are (there are no dates to realign); when the incoming events use weeks above the target's cycle, a question offers to **lengthen the target's cycle** to the source's (the default, offered when the source's cycle is longer; to a multiple, the target's own patterns and per-week times are extended periodically, as in R-CYC-10, unless the target has staged weeks), to **keep those weeks as staged weeks** of the target (its kept weeks grow to the source's, R-CYC-21), or the choices of shrinking for the occurrences in those weeks (removed, or moved into a schedule of their own per week, R-CYC-10); the source's own staged weeks arrive as staged weeks when lengthening; *Cancel* inserts or transfers nothing. One undo step in the affected workspace. Every-week events need nothing. Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-14** **[U]** **Data format:** FMT 9 added `cycle` (workspace, integer 2–4, absent = 1), `event.weeks` and `slot.wk`, together with `event.away` (R-EVT-24) since both were released at once (R-DATA-08; `MIG[8]` changes nothing, since older data has none of them). `parseData` validates them (integers only, `cycle` clamped to 1–4, normalisation of R-CYC-03). FMT 10 added the weeks' names, descriptions and colours (`weekInfo`, R-CYC-17). FMT 11 added the weeks of phases (`phase.weeks`, a phase's `slot.wk`, R-CYC-16; `MIG[10]` changes nothing: older phases are every week), validated and normalised exactly as for events. FMT 12 added `event.turns` (R-CYC-18; kept only as `true` or `false`; `MIG[11]` changes nothing: older events are automatic). FMT 15 added `pool`, the weeks kept, staged weeks included (R-CYC-21; an integer from `cycle` to 8, stored only when above `cycle`; `MIG[14]` changes nothing: older workspaces have no staged weeks). The import limit of 200 repetitions per event stays (routines no longer multiply) **[C✓]**.
- **R-CYC-15** **[U]** **Rotas are kept by people, not computed:** a rota (whose turn it is to cook, to drive) is written as **one event whose owner is whoever set the rota, with the people in the rotation as involved people and the procedure in the description** **[U]**; a fixed, stable per-week rota may instead use *Different per week* with a stand-in on each week's line. Overlaps with the involved people's other activities show as stripes, which mark places that need a decision, and are appropriate here **[U]**. Known limit, stated in the help: with *Include time as involved person* (R-STAT-01) the whole rota counts for each person in it. **Rejected:** a *Rotate between…* helper that assigned turns by availability and balance: rotas change too often, and a missed or swapped turn cannot be represented, so stored turns would drift out of date **[U]**. A help tip describes cycles and rotas. Turns are counted only where each repetition names its person (R-CYC-18).
- **R-CYC-16** **[U]** **Rhythms can differ per week (shift work):** **phases get weeks exactly like events** **[U]** (chosen by the user over a rhythm per week chosen in *Weeks in the cycle…*, which would have made the shown rhythm data instead of view state): a **week pattern** (`phase.weeks`, absent = every week, R-CYC-02) or **a week per time** (`slot.wk`, *Different per week*, R-CYC-03), with the same normalisation in `parseData` and after a save (`normWk`). One rhythm holds all its weeks, and **only one rhythm is shown at a time**, as before (R-PHS-02). Phases never cross midnight, so they have no week wrap. **Dialog:** the phase dialog has the same **Weeks** row (its tooltip speaks of phases and shift work, `wkRowTipPh`), *Different per week* and the **Week** column / week button of every time as the event dialog (R-CYC-08), on a computer and on a phone. **Grid:** the shown week draws only its own phases (`inWk`); lips group identical phases only within the shown week; a phase that is not every week names its weeks in its lip's and box's tooltip (`wkNote`); hit-testing, choosers and drags follow R-CYC-07/09 (a patterned phase shifts days mod 7, a phase with its own week shifts along the cycle). **Agendas:** the day agenda's phase headings (R-PRN-04) are the shown week's; the rhythm agenda (R-PHS-07) names a pattern's weeks and gives a phase with different times per week a section per week. **Cycle changes:** lengthening to a multiple *in step* extends phases' weeks too; shrinking lists the phases that lose weeks with the events and, when asked to keep them, **moves them into a new rhythm per removed week** (*Week 3*, not shown, as R-CYC-10 does with a schedule for events); inserting a workspace (R-CYC-13) and **transferring a rhythm** to another workspace ask the same question as for events when phases use weeks beyond the target's cycle. Staged weeks (R-CYC-21…23) apply to phases exactly as to events. Data: FMT 11 (R-CYC-14).
- **R-CYC-18** **[U]** **Rota turns are counted** (requested by the user after R-CYC-15): a **turn** is one occurrence over the cycle in which a person is **responsible** (a repetition's person, the owner included when the owner performs it); a repetition of a patterned event counts once in each of its weeks, one with its own week (R-CYC-03) once. **Which events count turns** **[U]** (not every event with several people is a rota that matters): each event has a mark, `event.turns` — **absent = automatic** (it counts when **two or more people** are responsible), `true` = always (also an event only one person does: *Ann 4 turns in the cycle*), `false` = never. The mark is **workspace data** **[U]** (undoable, an unsaved change, saved in files and links, carried by insert and transfer; format 12, R-CYC-14). `turnsOf(e[, slots, mode])` gives each person's turns per week, in People order, or `null`. A rota kept as an owner plus involved people (R-CYC-15) has no turns to count, which the tooltips say. Shown in two places, chosen by the user **[U]**: (1) the **event dialog**, a *Turns* line under **Who**: a list *Automatic (two or more people)* / *Count turns* / *Don't count* (`#fTuM`) and, while the event counts, the counts (*Ann 4 · Bob 3 turns in the cycle*; *turns a week* with a cycle of one week; *Nobody is responsible yet*), recomputed from the day table while it is edited (`updTurns`, from `updWarn`), their tooltip giving each week's turns (*Week 1: Ann 2 · Bob 2*); (2) the **statistics** (R-STAT-01), after the table: *Turns in rotas* (ⓘ tooltip) for one person, a row per rota of the visible schedules (Away excluded) with that person's turns per week (*W1 ⓘ* headers, R-CYC-17), their total and *Of all turns*; for *Everyone*, a row per rota with a column per person (our people, R-PPL-03) and *All turns* (external people's turns count in *All turns* only). Every rota row starts with a **✕** that leaves it out (`turns: false`), and **Add an event…** under the table opens a list, one event of the visible schedules per line (its colour and title; Away excluded; one left out says *left out*), like the type filter's list (R-STAT-07): picking one makes it count (`turns: true`; `tuAddPop`, `setTurns`). Both are undoable data changes and the statistics redraw at once. The section shows whenever the visible schedules have events, with *No rotas yet.* / *Bob takes no turns in a rota.* when its table would be empty, so the button can be found; the buttons are not printed. Copy table and the CSV end with the same block after an empty row. The unit (R-STAT-05) and the type filter (R-STAT-07) do not apply: turns are counts. **Rejected:** showing turns in the hover card and agenda **[U]**.
- **R-CYC-19** **[U]** **All weeks side by side** (a view of the whole cycle, every week's days in one row): an item **All weeks side by side** in the corner week menu (R-CYC-04), after *Week 1…n*, ticked while on, with a tooltip; chosen by the user over a Tools switch (out of reach on a phone) and a read-only overview dialog **[U]**. **View state** like the shown week (`allWk`): not undoable, not an unsaved change, not saved; choosing a week goes back to one week; off at a cycle of one week and in Live View. The corner button reads *W1–4* (*S1–4*). **Columns:** the days of week 1, then of week 2…, each header naming its week (*Monday · W2*) and carrying its week's colour band (R-CYC-17), with a stronger line where a week starts; the days not shown stay hidden (R-GRID-13). **In the day view** it shows that day in every week (*Monday · W1*, *Monday · W2*…), which also suits a phone. **Editable like the one-week grid [U]:** each column draws, lays out and finds only its own week's occurrences (R-CYC-07; the keys of the last drawn grid carry the week, `gk`, and the column under the pointer sets the week, `atCol`); a header opens the agenda of that day in its week; **a move across a week edge is a shift along the cycle** for a time with its own week (Sunday of week 1 → Monday of week 2), while a week pattern moves days as always (R-CYC-09); **creating in a week's column makes an event of that week** (its Weeks chips start with that week only). The now line is drawn in today's week's column. *N hidden* counts every week. **Print:** *Print this view (all weeks side by side)* prints the wide grid across the page (landscape) and *Print every week* still prints one week per page; a day view printed from an agenda is that day of that week.
- **R-CYC-20** **[U]** **Reordering the weeks of a cycle** (asked by the user: *would it make sense, is it possible?* — yes): in *Weeks in the cycle…* each week row starts with a **grip** (drag it, or focus it and use the arrow keys; `aria-label` *Move week 2*, tooltip), on every kept week, staged ones included (across the *Staged weeks* line it swaps a week in or out of the cycle, R-CYC-21), also while the length is changed (a week the new length adds has none); a moved row says *Week 1 (was week 2)* and takes its name, colour and description with it; a note explains what moves. **Apply** renumbers everything in one undo step (`reorderWeeks(ord, wi)`, `ord[i]` = the week that becomes week i+1): every event's and phase's week pattern, the week of every time (R-CYC-02/03, R-CYC-16) and the weeks' names, descriptions and colours (R-CYC-17). Consequences, stated in the note: a time that runs past Sunday midnight runs into the **new** next week (R-CYC-06), and an *every other week* pattern follows its weeks. **Today keeps its content [U]** (chosen over *today keeps its number*): the shown week and today's week (the Live View anchor, R-CYC-05) follow their week to its new number (`wkFollow`), except when their week leaves the cycle: then they keep their number and show the week that came in (R-CYC-21) **[C✓]**, and **undo/redo move them back and forth** (the undo entry keeps the order, `{s, wp}`). An order made before the length is changed is applied first, as its own undo step, then the new length (R-CYC-10).
- **R-CYC-17** **[U]** **A name, a description and a colour for each week** of a cycle longer than one week, so *Week 2* can say what is special about it (*At Dad's*, *Night shifts*, *Exams*). **Nothing is set until chosen [U]**: workspaces without them look exactly as before, and a 1-week workspace shows none of it. **Data** (format 10, R-DATA-08): `weekInfo` = one entry per week, `{name?, desc?, color?}` (`{}` = nothing set), trailing empty entries dropped, stored only while the cycle is longer than one week and something is set; `parseData` keeps strings only (trimmed; name ≤ 40, description ≤ 300 characters) and hex colours only, at most one entry per kept week, staged weeks included (`wiNorm`, R-CYC-21); a staged week's name is shown also beside a cycle of one week. It is workspace data: undoable, an unsaved change. **Editing [C✓]:** the *Weeks in the cycle…* dialog (R-CYC-01; full screen on a phone, R-UI-14) lists, under the length, a row per week of the chosen length: *Week 2*, a **colour swatch** (the colour picker; striped while there is none) with a **✕** that clears it, a **Name** field (*e.g. At Dad's*) and a **Description** field, each with an accessible name (*Week 2: name*); one **Apply** saves length and weeks together (one undo step), also when only the weeks changed. **Cycle changes (R-CYC-10) [C✓]:** shrinking keeps the entries of the weeks that become staged and drops those of the weeks removed; lengthening to a multiple *in step* (the existing checkbox, now also about names and colours; ticked by default, and in force while hidden) **repeats them periodically** (2 → 4: week 3 = week 1, week 4 = week 2), and the dialog shows those values for the new weeks until they are edited; any other lengthening leaves the new weeks blank. Inserting or transferring between workspaces (R-CYC-13) keeps the target's own week info (new weeks follow the same rule). *Leave out personal details* removes the week descriptions and keeps their names, like titles (R-SHARE). **Names [U]:** wherever a week is named, its name follows the number (`wkLabel`: *Week 2 · At Dad's*; short form *W2 At Dad's* in the statistics CSV and Copy table): the week menu, the switcher's tooltip and `aria-label`, the Weeks chips and the *Wk* menu of the event dialog, *Weeks 1 and 3* in the hover card, agenda and `aria-label` (with names it lists them: *Week 1 · At home, Week 3 · Exams*), printed titles and the day agenda (R-CYC-12), the statistics' week list and CSV (R-CYC-11), while the statistics **table columns and *Everyone* week lines show just *W2* with an ⓘ** whose tooltip gives the name and description (only when the week has either) **[U]**, the Live View week question (R-CYC-05) and Live View's day header (*Monday · W2 At Dad's*; just *W2* on a phone). **Descriptions** appear in tooltips and in the week menu, under the week's name. **Colours, all four uses [U]:** (1) the **week switcher** (R-CYC-04) is filled with the shown week's colour (`ink()` text) and each week in its menu has a colour chip; (2) a **4 px band under the day-header row** (corner cell included) in the shown week's colour (`.wkband` (on `#grid` and its print copy), `--wkc`), in the week view, the day view, Live View, on a phone and in print; (3) in the **event dialog** the Weeks chips have a coloured edge (filled when ticked) and the *Wk* button of *Different per week* is filled with its week's colour, with chips in its menu; the hover card and agenda show a chip before the weeks of an occurrence; (4) **printed titles** (the grid, *Print every week*, day views and agendas) get a band in the week's colour (`.wkh`), the statistics table's week columns and *Everyone* week lines get chips, and Live View shows the band. Colours are printed exactly (`print-color-adjust`). English and Spanish; the dialog's note explains the three fields.
- **R-CYC-21** **[U]** **Staged weeks** (*semanas en reserva*): a workspace may keep **up to 8 weeks** in all **[U]**, the cycle (1–4) plus **staged weeks after it** **[U]** (chosen over a cycle made of a list of chosen weeks, which would have shown *Week 3* in position 2): weeks 1…`cycle` are the cycle, weeks `cycle`+1…`pool` are staged. Uses: preparing a different week (exams, a busy season, a holiday at home) to **swap it in** later, and **shortening the cycle without losing a week** **[U]**. Staged weeks are kept but **never shown, printed, counted or exported**: the grid, Live View, the side-by-side view, agendas, print, statistics, turns, the ⚠ of overlapping repetitions, fixed lanes and the calendar file see only the cycle's weeks (`wksOf` gives only those; `onCyc`, `cycSl`), and a staged time never wraps into week 1. An event or phase that happens every week (no pattern) is in the staged weeks too; patterns and times may name staged weeks. **Data:** `pool` (FMT 15, R-CYC-14), stored only above `cycle`; weeks above it are dropped on import; `weekInfo` keeps the staged weeks' names, descriptions and colours (R-CYC-17). Workspace data: undoable, an unsaved change, carried in files, links and browser memory. **Weeks in the cycle…** lists every kept week; after the cycle's last week a **Staged weeks** heading with a note (*kept outside the cycle, ready to swap in…*) starts the staged rows, which are muted and say what was chosen for them (*Chosen for it: 2 event(s), 0 phase(s)*, on a line of their own under the fields). **Swapping [U]:** the grips (R-CYC-20) work across the line: dragging staged week 3 above week 2 makes it week 2 and the old week 2 staged week 3, everything chosen for it moving with it (`reorderWeeks`, one undo step). **+ Add a staged week** (tooltip) adds a week while fewer than 8 are kept, starting as the list beside it says (*Start the new week as*): **an empty week** (only what happens every week is in it) or **a copy of a kept week** **[U]** — its name, description and colour, the patterns that include it (they include the new week too) and its own times (repeated in the new week; `copyWeek`), so only what differs needs changing; the row says *Week 4 (copy of week 3)*, and the copy follows its week if that is moved in the same Apply; each staged row has a **✕** (*Delete week 3*): on Apply, a week that holds something asks first (*remove what happens in it*, the default, or *move it into a schedule — for phases, a rhythm — of its own*, `askGone`, `moveWeeks`), later staged weeks move down. Shortening the cycle keeps the weeks that leave as staged weeks by default; lengthening brings staged weeks back as they are (R-CYC-10). Inserting and transferring can keep weeks beyond the cycle as staged weeks (R-CYC-13). Help tip 23. English and Spanish.
- **R-CYC-22** **[U]** **Changes made in the cycle leave the staged weeks as they are**, while an event or phase stays one item across all its weeks **[U]**. **A week pattern belongs to the event [U]** (chosen over *never touch staged weeks*, which would split every routine into per-week times on its first edit): moving, resizing or editing an event with a pattern (or every week) changes its times in its staged weeks too. **Times of their own** (*Different per week*, `slot.wk`) in staged weeks are never changed from the cycle: drags of all repetitions shift only the cycle's times (along the cycle, R-CYC-09), resizes and margins skip them, *Applied to N* counts only the cycle's, and a grabbed time with no other time in the cycle moves alone. **The event/phase dialog** shows only the cycle's weeks: the Weeks chips and the *Wk* menus offer weeks 1…cycle; staged times are set aside on opening and saved back unchanged; a pattern keeps its staged weeks (unticking week 2 of an every-week event saves weeks 1 and 3); a new event ticked in every week of the cycle is every week. A note under the Weeks row says what the staged weeks keep: *Also in the staged weeks (Week 3): the times you change here change there too.* (a pattern) or *The staged weeks (Week 3) keep their own times, which are not shown here.* (different per week). Turning **Different per week** on gives the staged weeks of the pattern their own copy of the times as they were when the dialog opened; turning it off makes staged weeks with times of their own take the pattern's times, after the confirmation says so (*The staged weeks (Week 3) will take these times too.*).
- **R-CYC-23** **[U]** **Deleting an event or phase that also happens in staged weeks** asks first, and only then **[U]**: a small dialog (*Delete the event* / *Delete the phase*; *“Rota” also happens in the staged weeks (Week 3), which are not shown. Delete it there too?*) with its choices one per line: **Delete it from the cycle only; keep it in Week 3** (the first choice: a pattern becomes the staged weeks, an event with times of its own keeps only the staged times), **Delete it everywhere, staged weeks included**, and *Cancel*. One undo step. An event without staged occurrences is deleted at once, as before. Deleting a schedule or rhythm deletes its events and phases everywhere without asking.
