# Cycles of several weeks

- **R-CYC-01** **[U]** **A cycle of 1–4 weeks:** a workspace repeats every 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks (`cycle`, default 1, shared by all its schedules; stored only when above 1). Weeks are **numbered 1–4** (*Semana 1*) **[U]**, positions in the cycle, never calendar or ISO weeks: **the app holds no dates at all** **[U]**. The grid still shows **one week** at a time (the week switcher, R-CYC-04). Typical uses: a two-week rota, fortnightly activities, different days in alternate weeks. Set with **Weeks in the cycle…** in the workspace menu (R-DATA-01) **[C✓]** — it is workspace data (undoable, an unsaved change), not a per-browser Setting and not a Tools view; its dialog has a 1–4 list and explains patterns and *Different per week* in its tooltip. **Out of scope [U]:** month-based rules (*the first Saturday of the month*: months are not 4 weeks) and dated exceptions (holidays, a skipped week).
- **R-CYC-02** **[U]** **Week pattern of an event** (the normal case): `event.weeks` = the weeks in which **every** repetition of the event happens, e.g. `[1,3]`; **absent = every week** **[U]** — what all existing data, every new event and an event at cycle 1 mean, so lengthening the cycle never turns a weekly routine into a fortnightly one, and lunch stays 7 repetitions in a 4-week cycle instead of 28 **[U]**. Stored sorted and unique; a pattern naming every week is stored as absent **[C✓]**. Repetitions of a patterned event carry no `wk`. The peek card, agenda and `aria-label` name the weeks of an event that is not every week (*Weeks 1 and 3*).
- **R-CYC-03** **[U]** **Weeks per repetition** (irregular patterns, verbose by design **[U]**): every repetition carries `slot.wk` (1…cycle) and the event has no `weeks` — for Mon/Wed in week 1 and Tue/Thu in week 2, a stable per-week performer (a stand-in on that week's line) or a different time in week 3. **The mode is inferred, never flagged** **[C✓]**: an event is per-repetition when its repetitions carry `wk`. `parseData` normalises: in a per-repetition event a repetition without `wk` gets week 1 and `weeks` is dropped; pattern weeks and repetitions whose week is above the cycle are dropped (an event left without repetitions or with an empty pattern is dropped, like any event without repetitions) **[C✓]**.
- **R-CYC-04** **[U]** **Week switcher:** chooses the week the grid shows. **View state** like *Focus on one day* (R-GRID-11): not undoable, not an unsaved change, not saved, kept by undo/redo, clamped when the cycle gets shorter; the page starts on week 1 (or on Live View's week, R-CYC-05). **[C✓]** It is a button in the grid's **top-left corner cell** (`.dh.c`, sticky both ways, so it stays in view while scrolling, in the day view and on a phone), showing *W2* / *S2* (`aria-label` *Week 2 of 3, change the week*), and opens a menu listing the weeks (the shown one ticked; while Live View runs the live week is marked *today (Live View)*) and, except while live, *Weeks in the cycle…*. Hidden at cycle 1. **Rejected:** a sidebar control (invisible on a phone and while the sidebar is collapsed). **Focus ◀ ▶ and the phone swipe step through the cycle** **[C✓]**: Sunday ▶ = Monday of the next week (the last week wraps to week 1), ◀ the reverse; the day-view header adds the week (*Tuesday · W2*).
- **R-CYC-05** **[U]** **Live View's week:** starting Live View (R-GRID-12) with a cycle longer than one week **asks which week of the cycle today is in** (a small dialog, the shown week preselected; *Cancel* does not start Live View). The answer is **kept only in the page's memory** — never in the workspace data, browser memory, prefs, files or links — and is **asked again after a reload**, including the startup restore of a remembered Live View **[U]**. Live View then tracks the current time on the current day; **at midnight it moves to the next day, and after Sunday to Monday of week N+1, wrapping from the last week to week 1** **[U]** (worked out from the moment of the answer, held in memory, so a tablet that slept for days still lands on the right week **[C✓]**). **The corner switcher stays pressable in Live View** **[U]** (the one exception to the read-only grid): choosing another week jumps to today's day and now point in that week. Known consequence: a wall tablet that reloads (power cut, browser update) waits on the question until someone answers it **[U]**. **Outside Live View** the app does not know the current week **[C✓]**: the now line marks today's weekday in whatever week is shown (R-GRID-04), the switcher starts on week 1 (or on the live week if Live View ran in this page), and a phone opens on today's day view in week 1 (R-UI-13).
- **R-CYC-06** **[U]** **Week wrap:** the cycle is one timeline of n × 7 days. A repetition past midnight on **Sunday of week k runs into Monday of week k+1; the last week runs into week 1** (at cycle 1 this is today's Sunday → Monday wrap, R-REP-03). A patterned event (R-CYC-02) whose Sunday falls in week 1 only shows its continuation on Monday of week 2. Pieces, overlaps (R-REP-05), drags (R-CYC-09) and statistics (R-CYC-11) all use it.
- **R-CYC-07** **[U]** **Per-week rules:** occurrences in different weeks never meet. **Clashes, lanes, stripes and the ⚠ of overlapping repetitions** are computed from the occurrences of one week **[U]**; so are nesting (host and nested in the same week), background strips and their gutter, hit-testing, choosers, the peek card, Live View bubbles and *N hidden* **[C✓]** — the grid draws and finds only the shown week. The ⚠ of a repetition means it clashes in some week of the cycle. **Fixed lanes** (R-EVT-23): the check that gives an external person a lane is **cycle-wide**, so lanes do not jump when switching weeks **[C✓]**.
- **R-CYC-08** **[U]** **Dialog:** while the cycle is longer than one week, *When* has a **Weeks** row above the day table: toggle chips **1 … n** (all ticked = every week, stored as absent; at least one) and a **Different per week** switch (tooltips explain both, and that complex rotas are better kept as owner + involved people, R-CYC-15). **Pattern mode** keeps today's Mon–Sun table unchanged. **Different per week** hides the chips and adds a **Wk** column (a list 1…n, *Week*) to every time of the day table (R-REP-02), chosen by the user from three mock-ups **[U]**: lines are kept in day, week, start order; a day is ticked when it has times in any week; ⧉ copies all of a day's times **with their weeks**; switching it on copies each time into every week of the pattern. **[C✓]** A day's **+** proposes the same times in the next week that has none on that day yet (otherwise R-REP-02's rule: after the day's last time); a newly ticked day starts in the week shown on the grid; each time's week is chosen from its button's menu (*Monday: week 2*). **[C✓]** **Switching back to a pattern:** when every day's times are the same in each week that has any, the pattern becomes those weeks; otherwise a confirmation names the week whose times are kept (the first that has any) and says the other weeks' differences are dropped. Opening from a week-2 occurrence pulses its own week-2 line (R-UI-12). Under 560 px *Wk* joins the first line of each time's small grid. **Rejected:** a table section per week (28 day rows at 4 weeks) and week tabs (they hide the other weeks) **[U]**.
- **R-CYC-09** **[C✓]** **Dragging through the cycle:** a **patterned** event drags exactly as today (moves shift days mod 7 and the pattern is untouched: the weeks belong to the event, not to a repetition; changing one week means switching the event to *Different per week*). A **per-repetition** event's "all repetitions" move is a **true shift along the cycle** (Sunday of week 1 + 1 day = Monday of week 2; the last week wraps to week 1); Shift changes only the grabbed repetition, which is one occurrence; *Only this day* puts it back in its week; the "at most one time per day" check of R-REP-06 counts per (week, day); the nested carry (R-NST-03) follows its host's shift, week included. Moving a time to another week is done in the dialog (its *Wk*) or across Sunday midnight.
- **R-CYC-10** **[U]** **Changing the cycle length** (one undo step, nothing removed silently): **lengthening** keeps every-week events every week (only the Sunday overflow moves on to the next week) **[U]**; going to a **multiple** (2 → 4) **offers to extend patterns and per-week events periodically** (ticked by default: `[1]` → `[1,3]`, week-2 times copied to week 4) so "every other week" keeps its meaning **[U]**; going to a non-multiple (2 → 3) keeps the week numbers and the dialog says that "every other week" cannot be kept in that cycle **[C✓]**. **Shrinking** **[C✓]** lists the events that lose weeks (and those that would lose every occurrence) and offers: **remove those occurrences**, or **move them into a new schedule per removed week** (named *Week 3*, holding them as week-1 repetitions of copies), then removes what is left over; *Cancel* changes nothing. The shown week is clamped.
- **R-CYC-11** **[U]** **Statistics per cycle:** totals over the whole cycle plus **an average per week**, so fairness is visible **[U]**. **[C✓]** The statistics dialog (R-STAT-01) gets a week list, **Average week** (default) | *Week 1* …: the 168-hour bar, day bars and table show that week or the average; the table adds a column per week, *Average* and *Cycle total*; *Everyone* shows each person's planned hours per week; the CSV and *Copy table* get the same columns. For the average week the overlapping line counts the whole cycle (*Overlapping over the whole cycle: h:mm in N place(s)*). Hidden at cycle 1, where everything is as before.
- **R-CYC-12** **[U]** **Print, agendas and *N hidden* follow the shown week**, with an option to **print every week** **[U]**: with a cycle longer than one week, Print opens a two-item menu, *Print this week (week 2)* / *Print every week (one page each)*; in the day view, every week prints that day of each week **[C✓]**; printed pages, the day agenda and its title name the week (*Week 2*) **[C✓]**; *N hidden* counts only the shown week's occurrences.
- **R-CYC-13** **[C✓]** **Insert and transfers between cycles** (R-DATA-03/04): importing as a new workspace or replacing needs nothing (the record brings its own cycle). **Insert into the current workspace** and **copying/moving a schedule** keep week numbers as they are (there are no dates to realign); when the incoming events use weeks above the target's cycle, a question offers to **lengthen the target's cycle** to the source's (the default; to a multiple, the target's own patterns and per-week times are extended periodically, as in R-CYC-10) or the choices of shrinking for the occurrences in those weeks (removed, or moved into a schedule of their own per week, R-CYC-10); *Cancel* inserts or transfers nothing. One undo step in the affected workspace. Every-week events need nothing.
- **R-CYC-14** **[U]** **Data format:** FMT 9 added `cycle` (workspace, integer 2–4, absent = 1), `event.weeks` and `slot.wk`, together with `event.away` (R-EVT-24) since both were released at once (R-DATA-08; `MIG[8]` changes nothing, since older data has none of them). `parseData` validates them (integers only, `cycle` clamped to 1–4, normalisation of R-CYC-03); phases never carry a week (R-CYC-16). The import limit of 200 repetitions per event stays (routines no longer multiply) **[C✓]**.
- **R-CYC-15** **[U]** **Rotas are kept by people, not computed:** a rota (whose turn it is to cook, to drive) is written as **one event whose owner is whoever set the rota, with the people in the rotation as involved people and the procedure in the description** **[U]**; a fixed, stable per-week rota may instead use *Different per week* with a stand-in on each week's line. Overlaps with the involved people's other activities show as stripes, which mark places that need a decision, and are appropriate here **[U]**. Known limit, stated in the help: with *Include time as involved person* (R-STAT-01) the whole rota counts for each person in it. **Rejected:** a *Rotate between…* helper that assigned turns by availability and balance: rotas change too often, and a missed or swapped turn cannot be represented, so stored turns would drift out of date **[U]**. A help tip describes cycles and rotas.
- **R-CYC-16** **[C✓]** **Rhythms stay weekly:** phases have no weeks and are drawn the same in every week of the cycle (per-week rhythms, for shift work, are parked).
