# Cycles of several weeks

- **R-CYC-01** **[U]** **A cycle of 1–4 weeks:** a workspace repeats every 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks (`cycle`, default 1, shared by all its schedules; stored only when above 1). Weeks are **numbered 1–4** (*Semana 1*) **[U]**, positions in the cycle, never calendar or ISO weeks: **the app holds no dates** **[U]**, with **one exception** **[U]**: the date that tells this browser which week today is in (R-CYC-05). The grid still shows **one week** at a time (the week switcher, R-CYC-04). Typical uses: a two-week rota, fortnightly activities, different days in alternate weeks. Set with **Weeks in the cycle…** in the workspace menu (R-DATA-01) **[C✓]** — it is workspace data (undoable, an unsaved change), not a per-browser Setting and not a Tools view; its dialog has a 1–4 list and explains patterns and *Different per week* in its tooltip. **Out of scope [U]:** month-based rules (*the first Saturday of the month*: months are not 4 weeks) and dated exceptions (holidays, a skipped week).
- **R-CYC-02** **[U]** **Week pattern of an event** (the normal case): `event.weeks` = the weeks in which **every** repetition of the event happens, e.g. `[1,3]`; **absent = every week** **[U]** — what all existing data, every new event and an event at cycle 1 mean, so lengthening the cycle never turns a weekly routine into a fortnightly one, and lunch stays 7 repetitions in a 4-week cycle instead of 28 **[U]**. Stored sorted and unique; a pattern naming every week is stored as absent **[C✓]**. Repetitions of a patterned event carry no `wk`. The peek card, agenda and `aria-label` name the weeks of an event that is not every week (*Weeks 1 and 3*).
- **R-CYC-03** **[U]** **Weeks per repetition** (irregular patterns, verbose by design **[U]**): every repetition carries `slot.wk` (1…cycle) and the event has no `weeks` — for Mon/Wed in week 1 and Tue/Thu in week 2, a stable per-week performer (a stand-in on that week's line) or a different time in week 3. **The mode is inferred, never flagged** **[C✓]**: an event is per-repetition when its repetitions carry `wk`. `parseData` normalises: in a per-repetition event a repetition without `wk` gets week 1 and `weeks` is dropped; pattern weeks and repetitions whose week is above the cycle are dropped (an event left without repetitions or with an empty pattern is dropped, like any event without repetitions) **[C✓]**.
- **R-CYC-04** **[U]** **Week switcher:** chooses the week the grid shows. **View state** like *Focus on one day* (R-GRID-11): not undoable, not an unsaved change, not saved, kept by undo/redo, clamped when the cycle gets shorter; the page starts on **today's week** when this browser knows it, else on week 1 (R-CYC-05); switching to another workspace shows that workspace's today's week when known. **[C✓]** It is a button in the grid's **top-left corner cell** (`.dh.c`, sticky both ways, so it stays in view while scrolling, in the day view and on a phone), showing *W2* / *S2* (`aria-label` *Week 2 of 3, change the week*), and opens a menu listing the weeks (the shown one ticked; whenever today's week is known it is marked *Week 2 · today*, R-CYC-05) and, except while live, *Weeks in the cycle…*. Hidden at cycle 1. **Rejected:** a sidebar control (invisible on a phone and while the sidebar is collapsed). **Focus ◀ ▶ and the phone swipe step through the cycle** **[C✓]**: Sunday ▶ = Monday of the next week (the last week wraps to week 1), ◀ the reverse; the day-view header adds the week (*Tuesday · W2*). Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-05** **[U]** **Today's week (Live View's anchor):** the one date the app keeps, an exception to *no dates* (R-CYC-01) chosen by the user over asking again after every reload **[U]**. Starting Live View (R-GRID-12) in a workspace with a cycle longer than one week whose week this browser does not know yet **asks which week of the cycle today is in** (a small dialog, the shown week preselected; *Cancel* does not start Live View and remembers nothing). The answer is kept as an **anchor: the Monday of a week 1** (local date `YYYY-MM-DD`), **remembered in this browser per workspace** (`prefs.lvAnchor`, keyed by workspace id, R-DATA-06) **[U]** — never in the workspace data, browser memory of the workspaces, files or links; not undoable and not an unsaved change. **Today's week** = the whole weeks from the anchor to this week's Monday, counted in local calendar days (clock changes cannot move it) and wrapped by the cycle: so **at midnight Live View moves to the next day, and after Sunday to Monday of week N+1, wrapping from the last week to week 1** **[U]**, and a tablet that slept for days still lands on the right week **[C✓]**. **After a reload** a remembered Live View starts at once on today's week, without asking **[U]**; a workspace whose week is not known yet asks (Cancel turns the remembered Live View off). **Correcting it:** the corner switcher stays pressable in Live View **[U]** (the one exception to the read-only grid): choosing another week jumps to today's day and now point in that week **and re-anchors** (remembered); outside Live View the switcher only changes the week shown. **Per workspace [C✓]:** switching to a workspace while live shows its own today's week, or asks for it (*Show this week* / *Cancel*, which stops Live View); a workspace that gets a cycle while live asks at the next 30-second tick; *Duplicate* copies the anchor, *Delete* and *Clear memory* forget it; a cycle of one week stores nothing; changing the cycle length keeps the anchor (the week is worked out again with the new length; *keep in step*, R-CYC-10, keeps every-other-week events in line). **Outside Live View [U]:** the page opens on today's week when known (R-CYC-04), a phone opens on today's day view in that week (R-UI-13), the week menu marks it *· today*, the corner button's tooltip says *Today is in week N (remembered in this browser; to correct it, change the week while Live View runs)*, and the now line is drawn only in today's week (R-GRID-04); while it is not known the switcher starts on week 1 and the now line marks today's weekday in whatever week is shown. The date itself is never shown. Loading `prefs` keeps only well-formed entries (id up to 64 characters, `YYYY-MM-DD`, at most 200) in an object without a prototype, so no workspace id can reach `Object.prototype`.
- **R-CYC-06** **[U]** **Week wrap:** the cycle is one timeline of n × 7 days. A repetition past midnight on **Sunday of week k runs into Monday of week k+1; the last week runs into week 1** (at cycle 1 this is today's Sunday → Monday wrap, R-REP-03). A patterned event (R-CYC-02) whose Sunday falls in week 1 only shows its continuation on Monday of week 2. Pieces, overlaps (R-REP-05), drags (R-CYC-09) and statistics (R-CYC-11) all use it.
- **R-CYC-07** **[U]** **Per-week rules:** occurrences in different weeks never meet. **Clashes, lanes, stripes and the ⚠ of overlapping repetitions** are computed from the occurrences of one week **[U]**; so are nesting (host and nested in the same week), background strips and their gutter, hit-testing, choosers, the peek card, Live View bubbles and *N hidden* **[C✓]** — the grid draws and finds only the shown week. The ⚠ of a repetition means it clashes in some week of the cycle. **Fixed lanes** (R-EVT-23): the check that gives an external person a lane is **cycle-wide**, so lanes do not jump when switching weeks **[C✓]**.
- **R-CYC-08** **[U]** **Dialog:** while the cycle is longer than one week, *When* has a **Weeks** row above the day table: toggle chips **1 … n** (all ticked = every week, stored as absent; at least one) and a **Different per week** switch (tooltips explain both, and that complex rotas are better kept as owner + involved people, R-CYC-15). **Pattern mode** keeps today's Mon–Sun table unchanged. **Different per week** hides the chips and adds a **Wk** column (a list 1…n, *Week*) to every time of the day table (R-REP-02), chosen by the user from three mock-ups **[U]**: lines are kept in day, week, start order; a day is ticked when it has times in any week; ⧉ copies all of a day's times **with their weeks**; switching it on copies each time into every week of the pattern. **[C✓]** A day's **+** proposes the same times in the next week that has none on that day yet (otherwise R-REP-02's rule: after the day's last time); a newly ticked day starts in the week shown on the grid; each time's week is chosen from its button's menu (*Monday: week 2*). **[C✓]** **Switching back to a pattern:** when every day's times are the same in each week that has any, the pattern becomes those weeks; otherwise a confirmation names the week whose times are kept (the first that has any) and says the other weeks' differences are dropped. Opening from a week-2 occurrence pulses its own week-2 line (R-UI-12). Under 560 px *Wk* joins the first line of each time's small grid. **Rejected:** a table section per week (28 day rows at 4 weeks) and week tabs (they hide the other weeks) **[U]**. The week of each time has a **column of its own, headed *Week***, after *Days* on a computer, and on a phone card it heads the title (*Week [1▾] – Monday*, R-UI-07) **[U]**. Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-09** **[C✓]** **Dragging through the cycle:** a **patterned** event drags exactly as today (moves shift days mod 7 and the pattern is untouched: the weeks belong to the event, not to a repetition; changing one week means switching the event to *Different per week*). A **per-repetition** event's "all repetitions" move is a **true shift along the cycle** (Sunday of week 1 + 1 day = Monday of week 2; the last week wraps to week 1); Shift changes only the grabbed repetition, which is one occurrence; *Only this day* puts it back in its week; the "at most one time per day" check of R-REP-06 counts per (week, day); the nested carry (R-NST-03) follows its host's shift, week included. Moving a time to another week is done in the dialog (its *Wk*) or across Sunday midnight.
- **R-CYC-10** **[U]** **Changing the cycle length** (one undo step, nothing removed silently): **lengthening** keeps every-week events every week (only the Sunday overflow moves on to the next week) **[U]**; going to a **multiple** (2 → 4) **offers to extend patterns and per-week events periodically** (ticked by default: `[1]` → `[1,3]`, week-2 times copied to week 4) so "every other week" keeps its meaning **[U]**; going to a non-multiple (2 → 3) keeps the week numbers and the dialog says that "every other week" cannot be kept in that cycle **[C✓]**. **Shrinking** **[C✓]** lists the events that lose weeks (and those that would lose every occurrence) and offers: **remove those occurrences**, or **move them into a new schedule per removed week** (named *Week 3*, holding them as week-1 repetitions of copies), then removes what is left over; *Cancel* changes nothing. The shown week is clamped. Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-11** **[U]** **Statistics per cycle:** totals over the whole cycle plus **an average per week**, so fairness is visible **[U]**. **[C✓]** The statistics dialog (R-STAT-01) gets a week list, **Average week** (default) | *Week 1* …: the 168-hour bar, day bars and table show that week or the average; the table adds a column per week, *Average* and *Cycle total*; *Everyone* shows each person's planned hours per week; the CSV and *Copy table* get the same columns. For the average week the overlapping line counts the whole cycle (*Overlapping over the whole cycle: h:mm in N place(s)*). Hidden at cycle 1, where everything is as before. Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-12** **[U]** **Print, agendas and *N hidden* follow the shown week**, with an option to **print every week** **[U]**: with a cycle longer than one week, Print opens a two-item menu, *Print this week (week 2)* / *Print every week (one page each)*; in the day view, every week prints that day of each week **[C✓]**; printed pages, the day agenda and its title name the week (*Week 2*) **[C✓]**; *N hidden* counts only the shown week's occurrences. Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-13** **[C✓]** **Insert and transfers between cycles** (R-DATA-03/04): importing as a new workspace or replacing needs nothing (the record brings its own cycle). **Insert into the current workspace** and **copying/moving a schedule** keep week numbers as they are (there are no dates to realign); when the incoming events use weeks above the target's cycle, a question offers to **lengthen the target's cycle** to the source's (the default; to a multiple, the target's own patterns and per-week times are extended periodically, as in R-CYC-10) or the choices of shrinking for the occurrences in those weeks (removed, or moved into a schedule of their own per week, R-CYC-10); *Cancel* inserts or transfers nothing. One undo step in the affected workspace. Every-week events need nothing. Week names, descriptions and colours: R-CYC-17.
- **R-CYC-14** **[U]** **Data format:** FMT 9 added `cycle` (workspace, integer 2–4, absent = 1), `event.weeks` and `slot.wk`, together with `event.away` (R-EVT-24) since both were released at once (R-DATA-08; `MIG[8]` changes nothing, since older data has none of them). `parseData` validates them (integers only, `cycle` clamped to 1–4, normalisation of R-CYC-03); phases never carry a week (R-CYC-16). FMT 10 added the weeks' names, descriptions and colours (`weekInfo`, R-CYC-17). The import limit of 200 repetitions per event stays (routines no longer multiply) **[C✓]**.
- **R-CYC-15** **[U]** **Rotas are kept by people, not computed:** a rota (whose turn it is to cook, to drive) is written as **one event whose owner is whoever set the rota, with the people in the rotation as involved people and the procedure in the description** **[U]**; a fixed, stable per-week rota may instead use *Different per week* with a stand-in on each week's line. Overlaps with the involved people's other activities show as stripes, which mark places that need a decision, and are appropriate here **[U]**. Known limit, stated in the help: with *Include time as involved person* (R-STAT-01) the whole rota counts for each person in it. **Rejected:** a *Rotate between…* helper that assigned turns by availability and balance: rotas change too often, and a missed or swapped turn cannot be represented, so stored turns would drift out of date **[U]**. A help tip describes cycles and rotas. Turns are counted only where each repetition names its person (R-CYC-18).
- **R-CYC-16** **[C✓]** **Rhythms stay weekly:** phases have no weeks and are drawn the same in every week of the cycle (per-week rhythms, for shift work, are parked).
- **R-CYC-18** **[U]** **Rota turns are counted** (requested by the user after R-CYC-15): a **turn** is one occurrence over the cycle in which a person is **responsible** (a repetition's person, the owner included when the owner performs it); a repetition of a patterned event counts once in each of its weeks, one with its own week (R-CYC-03) once. Only events in which **two or more people** are responsible are rotas (`turnsOf(e[, slots])` → each person's turns per week, in People order, or `null`); a rota kept as an owner plus involved people (R-CYC-15) has no turns to count, which the tooltips say. Shown in two places, chosen by the user **[U]**: (1) the **event dialog**, a *Turns* line under **Who** (*Ann 4 · Bob 3 turns in the cycle*; *turns a week* with a cycle of one week), recomputed from the day table while it is edited (`updTurns`, from `updWarn`), its tooltip giving each week's turns (*Week 1: Ann 2 · Bob 2*), hidden while fewer than two people have turns; (2) the **statistics** (R-STAT-01), after the table: *Turns in rotas* (ⓘ tooltip) for one person, a row per rota of the visible schedules (Away excluded) with that person's turns per week (*W1 ⓘ* headers, R-CYC-17), their total and *Of all turns*; for *Everyone*, a row per rota with a column per person (our people, R-PPL-03) and *All turns* (external people's turns count in *All turns* only). Copy table and the CSV end with the same block after an empty row. The unit (R-STAT-05) and the type filter (R-STAT-07) do not apply: turns are counts. **Rejected:** showing turns in the hover card and agenda **[U]**.
- **R-CYC-17** **[U]** **A name, a description and a colour for each week** of a cycle longer than one week, so *Week 2* can say what is special about it (*At Dad's*, *Night shifts*, *Exams*). **Nothing is set until chosen [U]**: workspaces without them look exactly as before, and a 1-week workspace shows none of it. **Data** (format 10, R-DATA-08): `weekInfo` = one entry per week, `{name?, desc?, color?}` (`{}` = nothing set), trailing empty entries dropped, stored only while the cycle is longer than one week and something is set; `parseData` keeps strings only (trimmed; name ≤ 40, description ≤ 300 characters) and hex colours only, at most `cycle` entries (`wiNorm`). It is workspace data: undoable, an unsaved change. **Editing [C✓]:** the *Weeks in the cycle…* dialog (R-CYC-01; full screen on a phone, R-UI-14) lists, under the length, a row per week of the chosen length: *Week 2*, a **colour swatch** (the colour picker; striped while there is none) with a **✕** that clears it, a **Name** field (*e.g. At Dad's*) and a **Description** field, each with an accessible name (*Week 2: name*); one **Apply** saves length and weeks together (one undo step), also when only the weeks changed. **Cycle changes (R-CYC-10) [C✓]:** shrinking drops the entries of the weeks that go; lengthening to a multiple *in step* (the existing checkbox, now also about names and colours; ticked by default, and in force while hidden) **repeats them periodically** (2 → 4: week 3 = week 1, week 4 = week 2), and the dialog shows those values for the new weeks until they are edited; any other lengthening leaves the new weeks blank. Inserting or transferring between workspaces (R-CYC-13) keeps the target's own week info (new weeks follow the same rule). *Leave out personal details* removes the week descriptions and keeps their names, like titles (R-SHARE). **Names [U]:** wherever a week is named, its name follows the number (`wkLabel`: *Week 2 · At Dad's*; short form *W2 At Dad's* in the statistics CSV and Copy table): the week menu, the switcher's tooltip and `aria-label`, the Weeks chips and the *Wk* menu of the event dialog, *Weeks 1 and 3* in the hover card, agenda and `aria-label` (with names it lists them: *Week 1 · At home, Week 3 · Exams*), printed titles and the day agenda (R-CYC-12), the statistics' week list and CSV (R-CYC-11), while the statistics **table columns and *Everyone* week lines show just *W2* with an ⓘ** whose tooltip gives the name and description (only when the week has either) **[U]**, the Live View week question (R-CYC-05) and Live View's day header (*Monday · W2 At Dad's*; just *W2* on a phone). **Descriptions** appear in tooltips and in the week menu, under the week's name. **Colours, all four uses [U]:** (1) the **week switcher** (R-CYC-04) is filled with the shown week's colour (`ink()` text) and each week in its menu has a colour chip; (2) a **4 px band under the day-header row** (corner cell included) in the shown week's colour (`.wkband` (on `#grid` and its print copy), `--wkc`), in the week view, the day view, Live View, on a phone and in print; (3) in the **event dialog** the Weeks chips have a coloured edge (filled when ticked) and the *Wk* button of *Different per week* is filled with its week's colour, with chips in its menu; the hover card and agenda show a chip before the weeks of an occurrence; (4) **printed titles** (the grid, *Print every week*, day views and agendas) get a band in the week's colour (`.wkh`), the statistics table's week columns and *Everyone* week lines get chips, and Live View shows the band. Colours are printed exactly (`print-color-adjust`). English and Spanish; the dialog's note explains the three fields.
