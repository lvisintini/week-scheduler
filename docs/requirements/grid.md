# Calendar grid

- **R-GRID-01** **[U]** Columns Monday→Sunday, left to right. Days are **not** attached to dates (it's a repeating week; a workspace may repeat every 2–4 weeks, and the grid then shows one week of the cycle, R-CYC-01/04 — still without dates).
- **R-GRID-02** **[U]** **Hours shown** (*Horas visibles*): hourly rows from 05:00 to midnight by default. The useful hours depend on the workspace (a child's school day needs about 08:00–17:00, a shift rota the whole day) **[U]**, so the grid shows a **range of whole hours chosen with two knobs** in the **View** section (R-GRID-07), under *Days shown* (R-GRID-13), whose knobs work the same way; it replaced Settings' *Show early hours* **[U]**. **Remembered per workspace in this browser [U]** (`prefs.hours`, `{workspace id: [first hour, the hour the grid ends]}`, `0 ≤ first < end ≤ 24`; no entry = the default 05:00–00:00, and putting the knobs back there drops the entry), like `lvAnchor`: never in the file, not undoable, not an unsaved change; duplicating a workspace copies its entry, deleting one drops it, Clear memory drops them all; a stored value that makes no sense is dropped at start. **The knobs:** two range inputs on one track (0–24, step 1), each with its own accessible name (*Start of the hours shown* / *End of the hours shown*) and its value read as a time (`aria-valuetext`), dragged by mouse or finger (only the knobs take the pointer; 24 px knobs on a phone) or moved with the arrow keys; they **cannot cross** (at least an hour apart); the start knob lies on top in the right half of the track, so two knobs close together can still be parted; the value reads `05:00–00:00` (AM/PM follows the clock, and midnight is 00:00, R-GRID-05); the explanation (with a household and a workplace example) is the row's tooltip. **Rejected [U]:** *Fit to what's shown*, a button that set the knobs once to whole hours around the events on the grid: the knobs are quick enough on their own, and its name read like a third *Fit* switch. **Drawing:** events, strips and phases (with their lips) that cross either end are cut there, their hidden part kept and not drawn; a box cut at the end has no resize handle there, and a flexible core cut at the end shows neither its end margin nor its edge handle; what starts at or after the end is not drawn, and the grid scrolls no further than its last hour. With *Fit height* on, the hours shown fill the window. Printing uses the same hours. Events in hours not shown count in the *N hidden* pill (R-GRID-09); phases never do (R-PHS-05).
- **R-GRID-03** **[U]** **Saturday and Sunday: only the header cells have a different hue** (not the whole column). The hue covers just the header cell *between the day column's borders* — it must not extend into the phase-lip lane on the left, **except** the lane between Saturday and Sunday (both weekend headers), which takes the same hue so the two headers read as one band (the lane between Friday and Saturday stays neutral; the single-day print drops the join). Dark-mode variant exists.
- **R-GRID-04** **[U]** Settings → UI: AM/PM clock (R-UI-03); which days are shown is *Days shown* in View (R-GRID-13), no longer a Settings option **[U]**. A red **now line** on the current weekday's column (updates every 30 s, default on, never printed), switched by **Show the "now" line**, the first switch in **Tools** after the Live View row (R-UI-11; moved out of Settings **[U]**), remembered by this browser (`prefs.nowLine`); its tooltip explains the rules for weeks. With a cycle of several weeks it is drawn only in today's week once this browser knows it (with all weeks side by side, in that week's column), and in whatever week is shown until then (side by side, in every week's column) (R-CYC-05, R-CYC-19); kept as it was when the switch moved, since the line means *now* **[C✓]**. Live View always draws it (R-GRID-12).
- **R-GRID-05** **[U]** Midnight is always displayed as `00:00` (`12:00 AM` in AM/PM mode), never `24:00`.
- **R-GRID-06** **[C✓]** 48 px per hour by default (the *Row height* default; *Fit height*, on by default, sizes the rows to the window, R-GRID-07), 15-minute snapping, hour lines, sticky day headers. Each day header shows an **agenda icon**; clicking the header opens that day's agenda **[U]** (of the shown week, R-CYC-12). The top-left corner cell holds the week switcher when the cycle is longer than one week (R-CYC-04). **Narrow week columns** (under 70 px, from the grid width measured before the grid is emptied — a layout of the empty grid would reset the scroll position; e.g. a phone) get a **short name** (*Mo Tu We Th Fr Sa Su* / *Lu Ma Mi Ju Vi Sá Do*, full name as `aria-label`) without the agenda and focus buttons (they would sit on top of the names); **a tap opens that day** (R-GRID-11), whose header has the agenda **[U]**.
- **R-GRID-07** **[U]** **View (grid size, days and hours shown, grid text size):** the sidebar's **View section** (*Vista*; it was *Zoom* until it also held what is shown **[U]**; collapsible like the others, `secZoom`; never in Settings **[U]**; sliders have `aria-label`s, explanations are tooltips) holds three blocks **separated by dividing lines [U]**, in this order: the **sizes** — **Column width** (px per day, **70–1000**, default 110) and **Row height** (px per hour, 24–120, default 48), each a label line holding the label, a short **Fit** switch (*Ajustar*; its accessible name is the full *Fit width* / *Fit height*, `fitW` / `fitH`, default on, the explanation in its tooltip) and the value, with the slider under it at full width **[U]** (side by side on one line would leave the slider about 55 px in a 230 px sidebar); what is **shown** — **Days shown** (R-GRID-13) and **Hours shown** (R-GRID-02); the **text** — the **Text size** (`txt`, 80–160 %, step 10, default 100) with the **Show descriptions** switch (R-EVT-25) under its slider, together and without a line, since both are about the text in the boxes; the section ends there **[U]**. *Reset view* acts on the whole section, so it is **the section header's own button [U]**, at the right end of the header line where the other sections have their + (an icon, `ICON_RESET`, a circle back to its start rather than the undo arrow; accessible name *Reset view*; shown also while the section is folded) (*Restablecer vista*: both Fit switches on, 48 px, 110 px, 100 %, and this workspace's whole week and hours 05:00–00:00; its tooltip says so). Every row starts at the same left edge **[U]**. **Rejected [U]:** a mark on the slider at the fitted size, with the knob on it meaning *Fit*, instead of the switch: the fitted size can lie outside the slider (columns down to 40 px, rows outside 24–120), the mark is hard to hit (or snapping to it takes away the sizes next to it), it moves with the window, and keyboard and screen-reader users could not tell; the switch on the label line takes no extra room. **Fit width** on = the columns share the window width (40 px minimum, scroll sideways below that); off = each column is **exactly** the slider width (a narrower week leaves empty space on the right, a wider one scrolls sideways — that is how to make room for side-by-side lanes, R-EVT-10). **Rejected:** *Fill the window width* ("at least the slider width, stretched"): every value below window ÷ 7 looked the same **[U]**. **Fit height** on = rows sized so the visible hours fill the window height under the **measured** day header (it grows with the text; measured before the grid is emptied, since a layout of the empty grid would reset the scroll position; no layout → the slider value). A slider is **disabled while its Fit switch is on and shows the fitted size** (`updZoomUI` after every render; its tooltip says to switch off *Fit*), and **switching Fit off keeps that size** so nothing jumps **[C✓]** (a focused day is not a week column: unticking *Fit width* there keeps the slider value). **Text size** sets `--ts` on `:root` and `#grid`; grid text uses `calc(Npx * var(--ts,1))`: box text and its icons (and the room kept for the status icons), hour labels, day names, phase labels, the hover card and the Live View sizes (multiplied, R-GRID-12); the time column is `round(52 × ts)` px. **Lips and strip titles keep their size** (fixed-width lanes); the sidebar and dialogs never scale — that is the browser's zoom **[U]**. **A phone-sized window has its own row height [U]** (600 px wide or less, R-UI-13): *Fit height* and *Row height* there read and set `fitHP` (default **off**) and `rowHP` (default **100 px per hour**, about 7 hours on a phone screen, so boxes are big enough to tap), so the computer's 48 px and fitted rows never squeeze a phone (all the visible hours in a phone's height were ~35 px per hour, too small to tap); *Reset view* there goes back to that (its tooltip says so), and the *Fit height* tooltip explains it. **A phone also has its own width [U]:** *Fit width* and *Column width* there use `fitWP` (default on) and `colWP` (default 110 px), and **Fit width follows the view**: a day view (startup, a short day name, ◀ ▶, a swipe, Live View) turns it on, the week view turns it off and keeps the column width, so the week scrolls sideways at that width; ticking or unticking it by hand lasts until the next switch of view; *Reset view* there goes back to on and 110 px. Per-browser prefs (`rowH`, `colW`, `fitW`, `fitH`, `rowHP`, `fitHP`, `colWP`, `fitWP`, `txt`, and `hours` and `days` per workspace, R-GRID-02, R-GRID-13; the retired `fillW`/`fit` are no longer read); recomputed on window/sidebar resize while a Fit switch is on; changing a size keeps the view centred on the same spot; **printing ignores the sizes and the text size** (48 px / 110 px / 100 % via `withPrintScale`) but prints the days and hours shown (the section's tooltip says so). The section affects only the calendar grid — the sidebar (and its narrow-screen behaviour) is deliberately unchanged **[U]**.
- **R-GRID-08** **[U]** **Scrolling:** the time column and the day headers are sticky (the top-left corner cell is sticky both ways) and the grid's scrollbars are always visible (`overflow:scroll`, styled scrollbars). `100dvh` is used so mobile browser bars don't hide the bottom of the grid. The grid's scroll area (`#wrap`) is **one tab stop** (`tabindex=0`, `role=region`, *Week grid (arrow keys scroll)* / *Cuadrícula de la semana (las flechas la desplazan)*, set in `setLang`) with a focus ring on keyboard focus only (`:focus-visible`), so the arrow keys and Page Up/Down scroll it. **Firefox** draws the scrollbars as the operating system says (overlay scrollbars that appear only while scrolling under Windows 11's and macOS's defaults): no CSS can force classic ones there, so in Firefox *always visible* depends on the OS setting (*Always show scrollbars* / *Show scroll bars: Always*); Chromium and Safari follow the styled `::-webkit-scrollbar`.
- **R-GRID-09** **[U]** An **"N hidden" pill** (bottom-right of the grid) counts only event occurrences that are not shown *purely because* they fall outside the hours shown (R-GRID-02) or on the days not shown (R-GRID-13). Occurrences that are even partially visible (e.g. 04:00–06:00 with the hours starting at 05:00, or Fri 22:00 → Sat 02:00 with Monday to Friday shown) are never counted, and neither are occurrences hidden by the people filter or a hidden schedule; with a cycle of several weeks only the shown week counts (R-CYC-12). Tooltip gives the days-not-shown / hours-not-shown breakdown; a click **opens the sidebar on View** (unfolding the section) **with the start knob of the hours shown focused** when hours hide something, and **the first-day knob of the days shown** when only days do **[U]** (when every week is side by side, the counts of all the weeks add up). Never shown in Live View (R-GRID-12).
- **R-GRID-10** **[C✓]** Layer order in a day column: phase backgrounds → background-activity bands (R-EVT-17) → hour lines (drawn *over* phases, **[U]**) → events → overlap stripes (in the day column, over the boxes' colours, under all event text) → nested boxes (R-NST-02) → now line.
- **R-GRID-11** **[U]** **Focus on one day** ("laser focus"): each day header has a **focus button** (viewfinder icon, at the header's right; **shown on hover** and on keyboard focus, always on touch screens, so it doesn't make the day names wrap; tooltip *Show only Tuesday…* explains the way back); clicking the day name still opens the agenda (R-GRID-06). In focus only that day is drawn, **filling the window width** even with *Fit width* off (the row height, *Fit height* and text size still apply). The header shows **◀ day name ▶** (wrap around Sunday→Monday — with a cycle of several weeks into the next week, and the header adds the week, R-CYC-04 —, skip the days not shown, R-GRID-13; a focused day is drawn even when it is not one of them) and a **Week** button that brings the week back, as does **Esc** when no event is selected (Esc first clears a selection; ignored while a dialog or menu is open). **[C✓]** View state that is **not remembered** (a reload shows the week; the exceptions are Live View, R-GRID-12, which has no arrows, Week or agenda buttons, and a phone-sized window, which opens on today's day view, R-UI-13), not undoable, not an unsaved change. The **N hidden** pill counts only the focused day's occurrences in the hidden early hours. **Print** prints what is on screen: the day view while focused, the week otherwise; the focus buttons never print. Everything else works as usual (create, move, resize, peek, choosers); moving an event to another day or resizing into the next day's column is not possible in focus — use the dialog. A help tip describes it.
- **R-GRID-13** **[U]** **Days shown** (*Días visibles*): the grid shows **a run of days** chosen with **two knobs** in the **View** section (R-GRID-07), above *Hours shown*: Monday to Sunday by default, any run from one day to the whole week (Monday to Friday for the school week or an office, Tuesday to Saturday for a shop); it replaced Settings' *Hide Saturday and Sunday* **[U]**. **Remembered per workspace in this browser [U]** (`prefs.days`, `{workspace id: [first day, last day]}`, Monday 0 to Sunday 6, `first ≤ last`; no entry = the whole week, and putting the knobs back there drops the entry), like `hours`: never in the file, not undoable, not an unsaved change; duplicating a workspace copies its entry, deleting one drops it, Clear memory drops them all, *Reset view* drops the current one; a stored value that makes no sense is dropped at start; the retired `hideWeekend` is never read, so a browser that hid the weekend shows it again once **[U]**. **The knobs** work like those of the hours shown (one track, 0–6, step 1; their own accessible names *First day shown* / *Last day shown*, their values read as full day names; mouse, finger or arrow keys; the start knob on top in the right half): they **cannot cross but may meet** (one day); the value reads *Mon–Fri* (one day: *Wed*), and the short day names (*Mo Tu…* / *Lu Ma…*, R-GRID-06) sit under the knobs' places. **The grid** draws only those days, in order; the weekend hue stays on whichever of Saturday and Sunday are shown (the join only when both are, R-GRID-03); events on the other days are kept and counted in the *N hidden* pill (R-GRID-09); printing shows the same days. A focused day (R-GRID-11), Live View's today (R-GRID-12) and a phone's day view (R-UI-13) are drawn even when that day is not one of them; ◀ ▶ and swipes then go on to the days shown. The statistics count every day (R-STAT-02).

Live View (R-GRID-12) is in `live-view.md`.
