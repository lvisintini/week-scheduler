# Weekly statistics

- **R-STAT-01** **[U]** **Statistics dialog**: opened from the **chart button** (7th toolbar button) or a person's ⋯ menu → **Statistics…** (opens on that person); without people a toast explains that people are needed. Wide dialog (`xdlg({wide})`) with a **person picker** (every person except external ones (R-PPL-03), then **Everyone** **[U]**) and the toggle **Include time as involved person** (default on **[U]**); the last view and toggle are remembered while the page is open. Messages (copied, downloaded) appear in a status line inside the dialog, because a toast would be hidden behind it. Nothing is stored; everything is computed on opening. With a cycle of several weeks the dialog adds a week list (**Average week** | *Week 1* …, R-CYC-11).
- **R-STAT-02** **[U]** **Counting**: occurrences of the **visible schedules only** where the person is responsible (performs it) or (toggle) involved — the occurrence's effective list, R-EVT-21 — (not when on call, R-EVT-16); **events without any people count for nobody** **[U]**, and events marked *In the background* (R-EVT-19) count for nobody at all (an event with involved people but no responsible person counts for the involved ones). **Every hour of the week counts**, including hidden early hours and a hidden weekend; the people filter is irrelevant. Time past midnight counts on the day it falls on (Sunday → Monday, of the next week of a cycle, R-CYC-06). With a cycle of several weeks each week is counted on its own and the *Average week* is their mean (R-CYC-11). Away time (R-EVT-24) is its own line and is not *Not planned*. **Double-booked time counts once**, split evenly between the occurrences at that time **[U]**, and is reported separately as *Overlapping: h:mm in N place(s)* (a place = a continuous double-booked stretch). Events without a type → **No type**; time without events → **Not planned** (168 h − planned), shown **[U]**. Flexible start/end margins and repetitions *Around these times* (all margin, R-EVT-12) count as planned time and are reported as *of which flexible*, but a core beats a margin, margin against margin is split evenly, and only core on core counts as *Overlapping* (R-EVT-15); an *Around these times* that the person's other cores and Away time leave no minute of is squeezed out and counts as a core (split evenly, *Overlapping*); a nested one takes its host's time (R-NST-04).
- **R-STAT-03** **[U]** **Display** (inline SVG, no libraries; fully translated): one **168-hour bar** (types in list order in their statistics colours, flexible parts hatched, then *No type* grey, then *Not planned*), **seven 24-hour day bars** stacked by type, and a **table** (type with icon, hours `h:mm`, % of week, % of planned; *Planned* and *Not planned* totals) with the overlapping/flexible line underneath; a person with nothing planned gets a clear message. **Everyone** view: one 168-hour bar per person with their planned hours (with a cycle, also per week, R-CYC-11), plus a legend of the types used.
- **R-STAT-04** **[U]** **Export — both** **[U]**: **Copy table** (tab-separated, pastes into spreadsheets), **Download CSV** (`weekly-schedules-stats-<date>.csv`, UTF-8 with BOM, hours as decimal numbers; the status line says it is **not encrypted** and contains names and hours) and **Print** (charts + table, title naming the person, visible schedules/people line). The table behind the current view is `statsRows`; one person: a row per type (hours, %, per-day hours, flexible hours) plus Planned / Not planned / Overlapping rows; Everyone: a row per person, a column per type (all types), Planned, Not planned, Overlapping, Flexible. With a cycle of several weeks both add a column per week, *Average* and *Cycle total* (R-CYC-11); with Away time an *Away* row/column (R-EVT-24). **Spreadsheet formula injection** is neutralised (cells starting with `= + - @` get a leading `'`), CSV cells with commas/quotes/newlines are quoted.
