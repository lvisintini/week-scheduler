# Weekly statistics

- **R-STAT-01** **[U]** **Statistics dialog**: opened from the **chart button** (7th toolbar button) or a person's ⋯ menu → **Statistics…** (opens on that person); without people a toast explains that people are needed. Wide dialog (`xdlg({wide})`) with a **person picker** (every person except external ones (R-PPL-03), then **Everyone** **[U]**) and the toggle **Include time as involved person** (default on **[U]**); the last view and toggle are remembered while the page is open. Messages (copied, downloaded) appear in a status line inside the dialog, because a toast would be hidden behind it. Nothing is stored; everything is computed on opening. With a cycle of several weeks the dialog adds a week list (**Average week** | *Week 1* …, R-CYC-11).
- **R-STAT-02** **[U]** **Counting**: occurrences of the **visible schedules only** where the person is responsible (performs it) or (toggle) involved — the occurrence's effective list, R-EVT-21 — (not when on call, R-EVT-16); **events without any people count for nobody** **[U]**, and events marked *In the background* (R-EVT-19) count for nobody at all (an event with involved people but no responsible person counts for the involved ones). **Every hour of the week counts**, including hidden early hours and a hidden weekend; the people filter is irrelevant. Time past midnight counts on the day it falls on (Sunday → Monday, of the next week of a cycle, R-CYC-06). With a cycle of several weeks each week is counted on its own and the *Average week* is their mean (R-CYC-11). Away time (R-EVT-24) is its own line and is not *Not planned*. **Double-booked time counts once**, split evenly between the occurrences at that time **[U]**, and is reported separately as *Overlapping: h:mm in N place(s)* (a place = a continuous double-booked stretch). Events without a type → **No type**; time without events → **Not planned** (168 h − planned), shown **[U]**. Flexible start/end margins and repetitions *Around these times* (all margin, R-EVT-12) count as planned time and are reported as *of which flexible*, but a core beats a margin, margin against margin is split evenly, and only core on core counts as *Overlapping* (R-EVT-15); an *Around these times* that the person's other cores and Away time leave no minute of is squeezed out and counts as a core (split evenly, *Overlapping*); a nested one takes its host's time (R-NST-04).
- **R-STAT-03** **[U]** **Display** (HTML and inline SVG, no libraries; fully translated): one **168-hour bar** (types in list order in their statistics colours, flexible parts hatched, then *No type* grey, *Filtered out* (R-STAT-07), *Away*, then *Not planned*; drawn in HTML so its labels are not stretched, R-STAT-06), **seven 24-hour day bars** (SVG) stacked by type, and a **table** (type with icon, hours `h:mm`, % of week, % of planned; *Planned* and *Not planned* totals) with the overlapping/flexible line underneath; a person with nothing planned gets a clear message. **Everyone** view: one 168-hour bar per person with their planned hours (with a cycle, also per week, R-CYC-11), plus a legend of the types used.
- **R-STAT-04** **[U]** **Export — both** **[U]**: **Copy table** (tab-separated, pastes into spreadsheets), **Download CSV** (`weekly-schedules-stats-<date>.csv`, UTF-8 with BOM, hours as decimal numbers; the status line says it is **not encrypted** and contains names and hours) and **Print** (charts + table, title naming the person, visible schedules/people line). The table behind the current view is `statsRows`; one person: a row per type (hours, %, per-day hours, flexible hours) plus Planned / Not planned / Overlapping rows; Everyone: a row per person, a column per type (all types), Planned, Not planned, Overlapping, Flexible. With a cycle of several weeks both add a column per week, *Average* and *Cycle total* (R-CYC-11); with Away time an *Away* row/column (R-EVT-24). **Spreadsheet formula injection** is neutralised (cells starting with `= + - @` get a leading `'`), CSV cells with commas/quotes/newlines are quoted.
- **R-STAT-05** **[U]** **Hours or percentages:** a three-way switch in the dialog's bar, **Hours | % of week | % of planned** (default *Hours*; remembered while the page is open, `stU`; tooltip explains each) **[U]**, chosen by the user over a two-way *Hours | %* **[U]**. It sets the figures of the bar labels (R-STAT-06), the day bars, the table's **week columns** and *Average* (R-CYC-11), and the *Everyone* planned figures; the table's own *Hours*, *% of week* and *% of planned* columns stay as they are. *% of week* = share of the 168-hour week (of the 24-hour day in the day bars); *% of planned* = share of the planned time (in the day bars, of that day's planned time; the total above a day is its share of the week's planned time). *Not planned*, *Away* and *Overlapping* have no share of the planned time and show `–`. In a percent unit the *Cycle total* column is left out (it would equal the average), and *Everyone* shows planned time as a share of the week (of the planned time it would always be 100 %). Tooltips keep the hours and add the percentage (*Sleep 8:00 (46 %)*). **Copy table** and the **CSV** follow the view: the week columns are in the unit, their headers say which (*W1 At home (% of planned)*).
- **R-STAT-06** **[U]** **Labels on the bars:** every part of a 168-hour bar (one person's and each *Everyone* bar) that is at least **4 %** wide carries its figure in the chosen unit (R-STAT-05), in black or white (`ink()`) for contrast; narrower parts rely on their tooltip. The 168-hour bar is **HTML** (a flex row of parts, hatching as CSS gradients), because text in a stretched SVG would be distorted; it keeps `role="img"` and its `aria-label`. Each **day bar** has the day's planned total above it and the figure of each part at least about 2 h 12 min tall inside it. Printed statistics keep the labels.
- **R-STAT-07** **[U]** **Filtering activity types, in the dialog only** **[U]**: a button in the bar, *Activity types: all* / *Activity types: 3 of 9* (`.sttyb`, its tooltip explains the filter), opens **a list with one type per line** **[U]**, like the colour menu of a time in the event dialog (R-EVT-22): *Show all* and *Hide all* at the top, then each activity type and *No type* last, with its icon in its colour and a tick while shown (`menuitemcheckbox`, `aria-checked`). A click switches that type and redraws at once; the list stays open while ticking and closes with Esc or a click outside, scrolling when there are many types (`tyPop`). **Rejected:** a row of toggle chips under the bar, which did not work with many types **[U]**. The choice is remembered while the page is open (`stHide`), **independent of the sidebar eyes of the activity types** (R-TYP-04) **[U]**. The time of the types switched off is merged into one **Filtered out** part, a light hatched grey distinct from *No type*, placed after the shown types **[U]**, so the week still has 168 hours, *Not planned* is unchanged and *% of planned* still counts all the planned time. The table gets a *Filtered out (N type(s))* row, *Everyone* a legend entry, and Copy table / CSV a *Filtered out* row or column in place of the hidden types. Implemented as a view of the computed numbers (`stView`), so the counting (R-STAT-02) is untouched.
