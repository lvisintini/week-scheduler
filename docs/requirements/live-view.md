# Live View

- **R-GRID-12** **[U]** **Live View** (*Vista en directo*), for **a small screen (a tablet) left somewhere public so everyone can see what is going on now** **[U]**. **Controls in the sidebar, not on the grid** **[U]**: on a phone a floating **Live View** button (`#lvR`, R-UI-13), so it is one tap away — while live only that red stop button floats, with a floating **Now** (`#lvF`) above it after a manual scroll **[U]**; and, on a computer, a full-width row at the top of the **Tools** section (hidden on a phone; the computer's rail has no Live View button **[U]**, R-UI-01) (R-UI-11; not under the icon toolbar **[U]**) with **Live View** (`#lvB`, video-camera icon); while live it is red and reads **Stop Live View** (stop icon), and after a manual scroll a **Now** button (`#lvC`, crosshair) appears beside it; while live a **Full screen** button comes last, and on a phone it floats just above the stop button (R-GRID-15). Starting it **jumps to today's single-day view** (R-GRID-11) from wherever you are; with a cycle of several weeks it first **asks which week of the cycle today is in** unless this browser already knows it for the workspace (it remembers the answer per workspace), moves on to the next week after Sunday and lets the corner week switcher correct it while live (R-CYC-05). **The grid while live** (`#grid.lv`): **read-only: only scrolling, and tapping boxes to read them** **[U]** (and the corner week switcher, R-CYC-05) (capture listeners on `#grid` stop every pointer event before the boxes' own handlers — no dialogs, drags, new events, hover cards or right-click; resize handles hidden; `pointer-events:none` on the header and lip lanes; a press that moves less than 8 px is a tap, anything else is left to native scrolling); the day header shows **only the day name** (no ◀ ▶, Week, agenda or focus buttons, no agenda on click) **[U]**; **larger text** in the compact view (boxes 15 px, row 2 13 px, hour labels 13 px, day name 16 px, strip titles 12 px; all but the strip titles multiplied by View's text size, R-GRID-07) **[U]**; **tapping a box opens a speech bubble** **[U]** (nothing pops up by itself; **Rejected:** automatic bubbles for whatever the now line was over, and a separate cards column **[U]**): it **fades in quickly at the tap**, its triangular arrow pointing at the tap (up to 6 px off it where the column edge leaves no room, e.g. on a strip), and holds a larger hover card (`peekRows(it, true)`: title with person icon, time, type, flexible notes, *In the background*, *During* / *Also during this*, By, With / On call, description; no schedules, warnings or how-to lines) for **every occurrence under the tap**, found like any click (`piecesAt` on the events, lanes, strips, a nested box over its host, cores over margins): **where boxes overlap, one bubble with a section each** **[U]** (start order, divider, colour edge). **Several bubbles may be open** **[U]**, but **never two for the same occurrences**: tapping them again moves their bubble to the new tap and gives it a new minute, without fading in again (repeated taps must not stack duplicates) **[U]**; **each fades out a minute after it opened or was last tapped** (only a bubble's latest timer closes it) **[U]**, or at once with a **tap on it**, while a **tap on empty space closes them all** **[U]**. Open bubbles (occurrence keys, tap position as a fraction of the width and a minute) are redrawn after every redraw without fading in again; leaving Live View or midnight closes them. **Placement**: above or below the tap (never across its row), the arrow at the bubble's start, middle, end or as centred as the column allows, inside the column, plus tiers further away joined by a thin stem; the lowest score wins — another open bubble's area ×1000, the measured text rows of the boxes and strip titles (half weight over the bubble's own boxes), a penalty per tier, the arrow's distance from the tap, a slight preference for below and a centred arrow. Bubbles are 300 px wide at most (`--ax` sets the arrow), live in a `.lvl` layer of the day column (`aria-live="polite"`, *Happening now*) and scroll with the grid. **Following:** the now line is kept **a third of the way down** the visible grid (the top before the hours shown, the bottom after them, R-GRID-02), on the 30-second tick and after every redraw (zoom too), within the scroll limits; it **never moves while a dialog, a menu or a held pointer** is open; **at midnight** it moves on to the next day (also onto a day not shown, R-GRID-13; after Sunday, onto the next week of the cycle, R-CYC-05); the now line is drawn even when *Show the "now" line* is off (R-GRID-04). **A manual scroll** (one live view did not make) **pauses** following, shows **Now**, and **comes back by itself a minute after the last manual scroll** **[U]**; *Now* does it at once. **Bottom pill** **[U]**: a larger **● Live View Mode** pill (`#lvPill`, *Modo vista en directo*) with a **pulsing red recording dot** (`lvp` animation, off under `prefers-reduced-motion`); the *N hidden* pill is never shown in this mode **[U]**; beside it, the **people chips** (R-GRID-14). **Leaving: only** the pill, **Stop Live View** or **Esc** (not under a dialog or menu), back to **the week** **[U]** — except **on a phone** (R-UI-13), where the week is too wide: it stays on **the day view of the day Live View showed** (and its week of a cycle) **[U]**. The sidebar can still be opened, hidden and used **[U]** (the grid redraws and stays live; print prints the day view without the cards). **Remembered per browser** (`prefs.live`): a reload opens straight into today's Live View — the one exception to "focus is not remembered" (with a cycle of several weeks, on today's week, asking only if this browser does not know it yet, R-CYC-05). View state only. Tooltips explain each control; help tip 19.
- **R-GRID-14** **[U]** **People chips in Live View** (*Personas visibles*), so whoever is at the shared screen can show only some people's activities **without opening the sidebar** **[U]**: while live, a row of **chips at the bottom, beside the *Live View Mode* pill** (`#lvPpl` in `#pills`, `role=group`), **one per person of ours** (`ourPeople()`, in the People list order; **external people get none** **[U]**, R-PPL-03), each the person's coloured figure (`pIcon`) and **name** (ellipsised after about 12 characters' width) **[U]**. **A tap toggles that person** **[U]**: it is **the same eye as the sidebar's People row** (`person.visible`, R-PPL-01, visibility rule unchanged), so the two always agree, and like that eye it is **view state**: not undoable, not an unsaved change, kept with the workspace; **what is hidden stays hidden after Live View ends** **[U]**. A hidden person's chip is **faded with a dashed edge** (`.off`, `aria-pressed=false`). While one of ours is hidden an **All** chip (*Todos*, `.all`; named *All*, not *Everyone* **[U]**) comes last and opens every eye of ours again (external people's eyes are left as they are) **[U]**. Chips are finger-sized (at least 44 px high, R-UI-01), not animated, and their tooltips explain the toggle, the shared eye and the rule that keeps an activity shown through another person in it. **Placement:** right-aligned on the pill's line; rows that do not fit **go above**, the first people staying by the pill (`flex-wrap: wrap-reverse`); the pill keeps its one line; `#pills` is never wider than the space right of the phone's floating stop and *Now* buttons (R-UI-13) or, on a computer, right of the sidebar (`lvPplFit`, from `updLive` and the sidebar's resize grip). A tap on a chip is not a tap on the grid: open speech bubbles stay, except that **a bubble drops the occurrences that are no longer shown, and closes when none is left** (R-GRID-12). **Rejected:** tap to show only that person (solo) or a long-press for it, a filter of Live View's own separate from the eyes, everyone coming back by itself after a few minutes, round initials instead of names, chips for external people **[U]**.
- **R-GRID-15** **[U]** **Full screen in Live View** (*Pantalla completa*): a button that puts the browser in full screen (the Fullscreen API on the whole page, `requestFullscreen` on `<html>`), hiding its toolbar and bookmarks bar, for instance the roughly 150 px that Chromium's bars take on the kiosk's 720 × 1280 screen. **Placement: with the other Live View controls** **[U]**: on a computer the last button of the Tools row, after *Stop Live View* and *Now* (`#lvFC`, icon only); in the phone view a round floating button **directly above the red stop button** (`#lvFS`), so the column reads *Now* (after a manual scroll) · Full screen · Stop. It shows **only while live, and only where the browser offers full screen** (`document.fullscreenEnabled`; not on an iPhone). Its icon, label (*Full screen* / *Leave full screen*), tooltip and `aria-pressed` follow the browser's state (`fullscreenchange`), so leaving full screen from the browser (Esc, F11) updates it; after the height changes, following keeps the now line a third of the way down. **A tap toggles it; only that tap turns it on** **[U]** (browsers allow full screen only after a tap or click, so it is not remembered, and a reload into Live View is not full screen). **Leaving Live View also leaves full screen** **[U]** (the pill, *Stop Live View*, Esc). **Esc while full screen only leaves full screen:** the browser takes it, and if the key also reaches the page, Live View is not stopped; a second Esc stops it. A refused request changes nothing. **Rejected:** a button in the bottom row beside the pill, staying in full screen after Live View, and going full screen by itself when Live View starts **[U]**.
