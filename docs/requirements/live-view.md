# Live View

- **R-GRID-12** **[U]** **Live View** (*Vista en directo*), for **a small screen (a tablet) left somewhere public so everyone can see what is going on now** **[U]**. **Controls in the sidebar, not on the grid** **[U]**: on the collapsed rail a **Live View** button (`#lvR`, R-UI-01), so it is one tap away; and a full-width row at the top of the **Tools** section (`.lvrow`, R-UI-11; it used to sit under the icon toolbar **[U]**) with **Live View** (`#lvB`, video-camera icon); while live it is red and reads **Stop Live View** (stop icon), and after a manual scroll a **Now** button (`#lvC`, crosshair) appears beside it. Starting it **jumps to today's single-day view** (R-GRID-11) from wherever you are. **The grid while live** (`#grid.lv`): **read-only: only scrolling, and tapping boxes to read them** **[U]** (capture listeners on `#grid` stop every pointer event before the boxes' own handlers — no dialogs, drags, new events, hover cards or right-click; resize handles hidden; `pointer-events:none` on the header and lip lanes; a press that moves less than 8 px is a tap, anything else is left to native scrolling); the day header shows **only the day name** (no ◀ ▶, Week, agenda or focus buttons, no agenda on click) **[U]**; **larger text** in the compact view (boxes 15 px, row 2 13 px, hour labels 13 px, day name 16 px, strip titles 12 px; all but the strip titles multiplied by the Zoom text size, R-GRID-07) **[U]**; **tapping a box opens a speech bubble** **[U]** (nothing pops up by itself — automatic bubbles for whatever the now line was over were tried and dropped, as was a separate cards column **[U]**): it **fades in quickly at the tap** (`lvin`), its triangular arrow pointing at the tap (`LV_SLACK`: up to 6 px off it where the column edge leaves no room, e.g. on a strip), and holds a larger hover card (`peekRows(it, true)`: title with person icon, time, type, flexible notes, *In the background*, *During* / *Also during this*, By, With / On call, description; no schedules, warnings or how-to lines) for **every occurrence under the tap**, found like any click (`piecesAt` on the events, lanes, strips, a nested box over its host, cores over margins): **where boxes overlap, one bubble with a section each** **[U]** (start order, divider, colour edge). **Several bubbles may be open** **[U]**, but **never two for the same occurrences**: tapping them again moves their bubble to the new tap and gives it a new minute, without fading in again (found by the user on a phone, where repeated taps stacked duplicates) **[U]**; **each fades out a minute after it opened or was last tapped** (`LV_PEEK`, `lvTime`: only a bubble's latest timer closes it) **[U]**, or at once with a **tap on it**, while a **tap on empty space closes them all** **[U]**. Open bubbles (`lvOpen`: occurrence keys, tap position as a fraction of the width and a minute) are redrawn after every redraw without fading in again; leaving Live View or midnight closes them. **Placement** (`lvDraw`): above or below the tap (never across its row), the arrow at the bubble's start, middle, end or as centred as the column allows, inside the column, plus tiers further away joined by a thin stem (`.lvstem`); the lowest score wins — another open bubble's area ×1000, the measured text rows of the boxes and strip titles (half weight over the bubble's own boxes), a penalty per tier, the arrow's distance from the tap, a slight preference for below and a centred arrow. Bubbles are 300 px wide at most (`--ax` sets the arrow), live in a `.lvl` layer of the day column (`aria-live="polite"`, *Happening now*) and scroll with the grid. **Following:** the now line is kept **a third of the way down** the visible grid (the top before the visible hours), on the 30-second tick (`liveTick`) and after every redraw (zoom too), within the scroll limits; it **never moves while a dialog, a menu or a held pointer** is open (`liveBusy`); **at midnight** it moves on to the next day (also onto a hidden weekend day); the now line is drawn even when *Show now line* is off. **A manual scroll** (one live view did not make, `liveTop`) **pauses** following, shows **Now**, and **comes back by itself a minute after the last manual scroll** (`LV_BACK`, `lvRet`, `liveBack`) **[U]**; *Now* does it at once. **Bottom pill** **[U]**: a larger **● Live View Mode** pill (`#lvPill`, *Modo vista en directo*) with a **pulsing red recording dot** (`lvp` animation, off under `prefers-reduced-motion`); the *N hidden* pill is never shown in this mode **[U]**. **Leaving: only** the pill, **Stop Live View** or **Esc** (not under a dialog or menu), always back to **the week** **[U]**. The sidebar can still be opened, hidden and used **[U]** (the grid redraws and stays live; print prints the day view without the cards). **Remembered per browser** (`prefs.live`): a reload opens straight into today's Live View — the one exception to "focus is not remembered". View state only. Tooltips explain each control; help tip 19.
