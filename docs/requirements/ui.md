# Sidebar, settings, dialogs, theme, icons

- **R-UI-01** **[U]** All controls live in the sidebar (no header). Title with the favicon on its left at the top. Sidebar is collapsible (chevrons), its width adjustable (drag edge, 230–560 px), and the grid uses the free space. The **top row** (favicon, title, help, gear, chevron) **stays at the top while the sidebar scrolls** (`.sbtop` sticky, with the card background) **[U]**, so the chevron stays in reach on a phone. **Collapsed**, the sidebar is a 46 px rail holding the **›** that opens it at the top and, at the bottom, the **gear**, away from the chevron **[U]**; **no Live View button [U]**: on a computer Live View lives in Tools (R-UI-11, R-GRID-12). On a phone there is no rail: the buttons float over the grid (R-UI-13). At 600 px wide or less the top-row buttons are at least 44 × 44 px, with gaps. State saved in prefs.
- **R-UI-02** **[U]/[C✓]** Layout (user approved): top row = favicon, title (• when unsaved), help `?`, settings gear, collapse chevron; workspace switcher button (name, • if unsaved, menu; the menu also holds *Weeks in the cycle…*, R-CYC-01), Events|Phases switch, icon toolbar = **+** (new event, or new phase in Phases mode; accent coloured, tooltip also mentions dragging) **[U]**, undo, redo, save, load, print, statistics (R-STAT-01); collapsible sections **Schedules**, **People**, **Activity types** (R-TYP-01), **Rhythms** (count, ⓘ tip, + button), **Tools** (R-UI-11, with the **Live View** row, R-GRID-12) and, last, **View** (ⓘ tip and, instead of +, the *Reset view* button; the sizes, the days and hours shown, the text size and Show descriptions, R-GRID-07) **[U]**; slim rows (eye, coloured icon — click = colour picker, editable name, lock badge, ⋯ menu); every name field has an accessible name (*Name of the schedule* / *person* / *rhythm*; types: *Activity types*).
- **R-UI-03** **[U]** **Settings dialog** (gear): **three tabs** **[U]**, in this order — *UI* (the **AM/PM clock**, then **theme: System / Light / Dark**, full-width switch, and **language**; grouped because all three are about how the app reads, theme and language starting from the browser's settings; the clock moved here when the *View* tab went, its other option having become *Days shown* in the sidebar, R-GRID-13) **[U]**, *Data* (Restart, Clear memory, and the passphrase of browser memory: *Passphrase…*, or while protected *Change passphrase…*, *Lock now*, *Remove passphrase…*, R-DATA-11), *About* (R-UI-15) **[U]**; it first opens on *UI*; every option has an explanation. Tabs look like tabs (underlined active tab, no boxed button group; arrow keys switch) and the **dialog keeps one size**: all panes share one grid cell and inactive ones are `visibility:hidden` + `inert`, so the largest tab sets the size **[U]**. The last tab used is shown again while the page stays open (not saved). **Nothing about the grid's size or the days and hours shown is in Settings** — they live in the sidebar's View section **[U]**. Designed to gain more tabs later. **Help dialog** (`?`): *How to use*, R-UI-17 **[C✓]**.
- **R-UI-04** **[U]** Colour pickers: popover with swatches, HSL sliders, hex field and an **Apply** button (Enter also applies, Esc cancels) — used everywhere instead of `<input type=color>`.
- **R-UI-05** **[U]** Undo/redo (buttons + Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Shift+Z, 100 steps). **[U]** Visibility (eyes of schedules, people, activity types and rhythms), opacity, the shown week of a cycle (R-CYC-04) and view settings are *view state*: not undoable, not "unsaved changes". Lock *is* data; so is the cycle length (R-CYC-01).
- **R-UI-06** **[C✓]** Toasts: 2.2 s plain, 7 s with action buttons. Day agenda dialog has **Print agenda** (list) and **Print day view**.
- **R-UI-07** **[U]** **Event dialog layout — a sectioned form** (chosen by the user from three mock-ups; **Rejected:** one long column of labels): `#dlg` is 900 px wide (`min(900px, 96vw)`, so the day table rarely needs sideways room **[U]**); fields sit in a **label column** grid (92 px labels tied with `for=`), which folds to labels-above-fields under 560 px. **Only real values appear at event level** **[U]** (**Rejected:** a *By* list, a *Timing* switch and *All days* general times, which only set every day and looked like event settings). Top (no header): *Title \** + colour swatch (named *Colour*) in one cell, *Description*, *Activity type* (icon + list). **Who** (`.fsec` header with a rule): **Owner** (`#fOw`: *(none)* and our people, a person icon; the tooltip says it is the person who owns the task's conception, planning and execution, not necessarily the one who performs it, without naming Fair Play, R-EVT-20), **With** = the default involved people (our people only, never external ones) as **toggle chips** (each chip is a label holding a real, visually hidden checkbox; ✓ and accent when ticked, focus ring). **When**: with a cycle of several weeks, first the **Weeks** row (week chips and *Different per week*, R-CYC-08); then the day table (R-REP-02) with a header row (*Days · Start · End · Performed by · With · Colour · Timing*, and *Wk* after *Days* for *Different per week*; explanations in the tooltips), the Mon–Sun rows and their further-time lines. **Each time**: start, end, **Performed by** (*Owner (Ann)* — follows the owner, the default —, *Nobody (concerns everyone)*, or any person, external ones marked), a **Colour** swatch (R-EVT-22), a **With** button (*Default* greyed, following the event's list, or the day's own names; it opens `wPop`: *Use the event's list* switch + our people's chips), a **Timing** list (R-EVT-15) with the margins on a line of its own under the row, on a day row a **⧉** button that **makes other ticked days like this one** — all of them, the ones below or the ones above, from its menu (R-REP-02; toast *Copied to Friday*) and a **+** (another time on that day), on a further-time line a **✕**, and the ⚠ of overlapping repetitions. Day rows and further-time lines are boxes on one grid's columns (`.dr` is a **subgrid** of `#paneQ`, columns in `--dc`, time columns ≥ 68 px), so columns line up; each line's first cell restarts at column 1. **Day bands [U]:** a day and its further times share a light band on alternate days (`.dr.alt`), with a **darker 2 px line between days and a thin line between the times of one day** **[U]**, so the table reads day by day. With *Different per week* the week of each time has a **column of its own** after *Days*, headed *Week*, so it never sits beside the day checkbox **[U]** (R-CYC-08). **A long list of times scrolls inside the table** instead of making the dialog grow **[U]**: `#paneQ` is at most `min(420px, 50vh)` high with its own scroll bar, the header row stays put (sticky cells; three empty header cells and their shadows cover the ⧉/+/⚠ columns and the gaps; on a phone the header row is not shown, see below; the whole `.dhd` is sticky), and a time added with **+** is scrolled into view. **On a phone [U]** (600 px wide or less; the label column folds away under 560 px): each time is a **card**; unticked days take no room and the header row is not needed. **Adding and removing [U]:** a **+ Add a time** button under the cards opens a small menu — the **week** (with *Different per week*, R-CYC-08) and the **day** — that ticks that day (its first time, copied from the nearest ticked day as when ticking it) or adds another time on it, and opens the new card; **every card has ✕**: removing a day's first time moves its next time up, removing its only time unticks the day; the cards' + is not shown (**Rejected:** a row of day pills, which read like a filter **[U]**). **Title [U]:** *Monday* (*Monday, time 2* for a further time), and with *Different per week* **Week [1▾] – Monday**, the week button inside the title. **Accordion [U]:** a card folds to its header — a button (`.sum`, `aria-expanded`) with the title and a summary (*10:00–11:00 · Ann*, plus the timing when it is not fixed), ⚠, ⧉ and ✕ — and a tap opens its labelled fields (*Start*, *End*, *Performed by*, *With*, *Timing*, *Colour*; the margins line under them); **one card is open at a time** **[U]**: the card just added, the repetition opened from the grid (R-UI-12) or the only card. Phases get the same cards with start and end only. **How it fits in** (`#fFit`): the event-wide switches together — *Involved people are only on call* (shown while somebody is involved, by default or on some day, and the event is not *Away*; its value is kept), *Can be done during the person's other activities*, and **Drawn as: Box / In the background / Away** (R-EVT-19, R-EVT-24). **Schedules \*** (or **Rhythms \***) as chips; *at least one* is in the tooltip and the error. Footer above a rule: Delete / Copy… / Cancel / Save. On a phone the dialog fills the screen and shows these sections in three tabs (R-UI-14). Long explanations live in tooltips, not labels. Phases: no Who or How it fits in, no performer, With or timing; phase end times stop at 00:00.
- **R-UI-08** **[U]** **Light/dark theme** toggle in Settings (System default = follows `prefers-color-scheme`; Light/Dark force it via `data-theme` on `<html>`; colours are CSS variables, dark values duplicated in the media query and the `[data-theme=dark]` rule). The muted grey `--mut` (hour labels and other secondary text) has at least 4.5:1 on `--bg` and `--card` in both themes (light `#636b7d`). Red texts (Delete in menus, error messages, the ✕ that removes a time) use `--red`: `#d33` in light mode, `#ff7b7b` in dark mode, where `#d33` fell below 4.5:1. Text on an accent background (primary buttons, the selected Events | Phases button) uses `--onacc`: white in light mode, `#0d1220` in dark mode, where white on the lighter accent was only 2.9:1. Per browser (prefs), never in the save file; printing is always light.
- **R-UI-09** **[U]** Icons: calendar icon for schedules, user icon in the person's colour for people, the chosen built-in icon for activity types (R-TYP-02), calendar favicon (inline SVG).
- **R-UI-10** **[U]** **The event/phase dialog can be dragged aside** to look at the boxes it hides: by its **grip bar** (`#dGrip`, a full-width strip of six dots at the top, `cursor:move`, tooltip explaining dragging, the arrow keys and the reset) or by **any empty part of the dialog** (not fields, labels, buttons, chips, the day table or the menus inside it: `DLG_NODRAG`). Mouse and touch (pointer events; `touch-action:none` on the grip); the focused grip moves it **20 px per arrow key**. The grid behind **stays dimmed** **[U]** (the backdrop is unchanged). Placed with `left`/`top`, **not a transform**, so the fixed-position menus inside the dialog (colour, With, colour picker) keep their viewport coordinates; at least 120 px of it and its grip bar's height stay inside the window. **Every open puts it back in the middle** **[U]**. Not on a phone, where it fills the screen (R-UI-14).
- **R-UI-11** **[U]** **Tools** (*Herramientas*): a collapsible sidebar section **after Rhythms, before View** **[U]** (`secTools`, ⓘ tip) for Live View (its row is hidden on a phone, where Live View starts from the floating camera, R-UI-13 **[U]**) and temporary ways of looking at the week; **every switch is remembered by this browser** **[U]** (prefs); the switches here and in View are **sliding toggles** **[U]** (real checkboxes with `role="switch"`, drawn with `appearance:none`: a `--mut` track, `--acc` when on, a white knob that slides; focus ring; no animation under `prefers-reduced-motion`; the Settings dialog keeps plain checkboxes), none of them is data (no undo, no unsaved mark). It holds, in this order: the **Live View** row (`#lvB` / `#lvC`, R-GRID-12; folded with the section); **Show the "now" line** (`prefs.nowLine`, default on; moved here from Settings **[U]**, R-GRID-04); **Highlight events without an activity type** (`prefs.hlNoType`): the boxes and strips of untyped events get a **pulsing red outline** (an `outline` so it never fights the selection's box-shadow; a still outline under `prefers-reduced-motion`; never printed); **Colour by activity type** (`prefs.colBy='t'`) and **Colour by person** (`'p'`, the performer), which **exclude each other** and temporarily draw **every occurrence** in its type's or its performer's colour, **grey (the statistics' *No type* colour) without one** — through `colOf`, so boxes, margins, strips, stripes, hover card, Live View, agenda and print all follow. The stored colours are untouched: `colRaw` resolves them (R-EVT-22) and the event dialog's swatches keep showing them. Last, **Fixed lanes, one per person** (R-EVT-23).
- **R-UI-12** **[U]** **Opening a repeating event from the grid shows the clicked repetition:** a click or tap on an occurrence (also via the chooser, which passes the repetition picked or under the pointer) opens the dialog with that time's line (a day row or a further-time line; `o.src`/`x.src` remember each line's slot) **scrolled to the middle of the day table only when it is not fully visible** below the sticky header **[U]**, and the line **pulses once** (about 1.4 s, accent background and ring on its cells; a still highlight under `prefers-reduced-motion`; the class is removed after 1.6 s). Only for events and phases with more than one repetition; **+** and creating by dragging open no line. With weeks per repetition (R-CYC-03) it is the line of the clicked week.
- **R-UI-13** **[U]** **Phone-sized windows** (600 px wide or less, `phone()` = `matchMedia('(max-width:600px)')`; phones are for checking a schedule, `product.md`): the sidebar is a **drawer**. It **starts closed whatever was saved** (`prefs.collapsed` is set at startup, not saved); opened, it lies **over** the grid (fixed, full height, `min(90vw, 360px)`, shadow) above a dimmed backdrop (`#sbBack`) that closes it on a tap, so the grid keeps the full width; the resize edge is hidden. **Its top row is the computer's [U]:** the favicon centred to the left of the title (which may wrap), then **? · ⚙ · ‹**, with no Live View button (R-UI-02). **Closed, there is no rail [U]:** the grid has the whole width, and three round 44 px buttons float at the bottom-left over the time column, **? · Live View · ›** from top to bottom (help, Live View, open the drawer; the gear is in the drawer), clear of the pills and toasts at the bottom centre. **In Live View [U]** only the red **stop** button floats (help and › are hidden, so neither the drawer nor help opens from Live View), and after a manual scroll a **Now** button (`#lvF`, crosshair) floats above it (R-GRID-12). The Tools section's Live View row is hidden on a phone (R-UI-11): Live View starts from the floating camera. A phone **opens on today's day view** (R-GRID-11; `focusD = today()` at startup unless Live View is remembered, which wins; in today's week of a cycle when this browser knows it, else week 1, R-CYC-05): a second exception, after Live View, to "focus is not remembered"; ◀ ▶ and **Week** work as usual. **Swiping sideways in the day view changes the day** on any touch screen (left = next, right = previous, wrapping — into the next or previous week of a cycle, R-CYC-04 — and skipping the days not shown like ◀ ▶, R-GRID-13): it counts when the finger starts moving within the long-press time (so a long-press drag never swipes), goes more than 60 px and over twice as far sideways as up or down, and did not start within 24 px of the screen edge (the browser's back gesture); never in Live View; the day view's columns are `touch-action:pan-y` (`#grid.fo`), since nothing scrolls sideways there. Large dialogs fill a phone screen (R-UI-14). Desktop windows are unchanged.
- **R-UI-14** **[U]** **Large dialogs on a phone** (600 px wide or less, as R-UI-13; desktop and tablet windows are unchanged): the large dialogs **fill the screen** (`100vw × 100dvh`, no border or rounded corners, safe-area padding) with their **header and footer fixed** and only the middle scrolling, so Save / Close are always in reach. Full screen: the **event/phase dialog**, the **day agenda**, and the `xdlg` dialogs opened with `full:true` — **Settings**, **Help**, **Statistics**, **Copy…**, **Weeks in the cycle** (it lists the weeks' names and colours, R-CYC-17), the **calendar export** (its steps, dates and lists, R-SHARE-09), and the import people-mapping and transfer dialogs (long lists of people); the small ones (confirms, scope, passwords, Share/Save/Load, the Live View week question) stay floating windows **[U]**. The event/phase dialog **can't be dragged** on a phone (no grip bar, the drag and the arrow keys do nothing; R-UI-10) **[U]**. **The event dialog shows one tab at a time [U]**: a tab row **Event · When · More** (*Evento · Cuándo · Más*; a phase: **Phase · When · Rhythms**, *Franja · Cuándo · Ritmos*) — **Event** = title, colour, description, activity type and *Who* (owner, With); **When** = the Weeks row, the day chips and the day cards (R-UI-07), which fill the tab and scroll (the *When* heading is hidden, the tab says it) and the overlap warning; **More** = *How it fits in* and *Schedules* / *Rhythms* **[U]**. The error line and the footer are outside the tabs. Tabs look like the Settings tabs (underlined active tab, `role=tab`, `aria-selected`, one tab stop, arrow keys switch) and are at least 44 px high. **Every open starts on Event [C✓]**; opening a repetition from the grid (R-UI-12) scrolls it into view and pulses it when **When** is first shown (a hidden tab can't scroll). **When** shows **⚠** while the table has overlap warnings (R-REP-05); a **failed Save** switches to the tab with the field the error is about (title → Event; days, times, margins, Away without people → When; no schedule or rhythm → More) and marks that tab with a red dot until the next Save or open **[C✓]**. On a desktop the tab row is hidden and every section is shown, as in R-UI-07. Help text, tooltips and the rest of the dialog are the same on every screen.

Built from the scripts, so `index.html` holds no external address. The text is kept short because the largest tab sets the dialog's size (R-UI-03).
- **R-UI-16** **[U]** The **Save…** and **Load…** dialogs list their choices **one per line**, full width, in their usual order with the primary choice first and Cancel last (`xdlg({stack:true})` → `.btns.stack`, a column); other dialogs keep their row of buttons.
- **R-UI-17** **[U]** **How to use** (`?`, *Cómo se usa*; `openHelp`): **every tip** (`help1…25`) **in titled sections** (*Creating and editing*, *Repetitions and midnight*, *Overlaps and clashes*, *People and owners*, *Flexible time*, *Activity types and statistics*, *In the background, away and along the way*, *Looking at the week*, *Rhythms*, *Cycles of weeks*, *Saving and sharing*; each tip exactly once) **[U]**: the help stays the full reference, and the Examples (R-DATA-12) are the hands-on version **[U]**. `help24` explains owners, stand-ins and a day's own With; `help25` the three kinds of timing **[C✓]**. **At the top:** one intro line and **Open the examples** (tooltip as in the workspace menu), which closes the dialog and then opens the Examples **[U]**. **Topics:** a list of the sections (`nav`, *Topics*) whose buttons scroll a section into view (buttons, not `#` links, since links use the address) **[C✓]**. **Size [U]:** on a computer the dialog is large (`#xDlg.howto`: `min(1100px, 94vw)` × `min(88vh, 1000px)`), with only the middle scrolling, so the title and Close stay put; **from 900 px wide** the topics are a column on the left that stays in view (`position:sticky`) beside the sections; narrower, they wrap above them; **on a phone** the dialog fills the screen as R-UI-14 says and the topics are hidden (the sections follow one another). Nothing scrolls sideways at any width. **Pictures [U]:** ten sections (all but *Cycles of weeks*, which the one-week Examples cannot show) end with a picture of the Examples workspace in the interface language (`img/help/<en|es>/<name>.png`, `HELP` in `js/model.js` holds name, alt text and size): **under the section's tips**, at its own size up to the section's width — side by side with the tips they were too small to read **[C✓]**; `loading="lazy"`, `width`/`height` set so nothing jumps; alt texts in both languages say what the picture shows. They are **screenshots taken by `npm run shots`** (`tests/shots/`: Playwright, from the Examples in a light theme at 1×, the phone picture at 1.5×; about 1.4 MB for both languages), committed with the code and taken again after a change that alters what they show (`docs/known-issues.md`: they can go stale and are light-themed in the dark theme). Serving them needed `img-src 'self'` (R-SEC-01).
