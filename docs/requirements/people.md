# People

- **R-PPL-01** **[U]** A People list "created in the same fashion as schedules": name, colour, icon, eye (show/hide). **Visibility rule (`slotVisible`, on the occurrence's performer and its effective involved people, R-EVT-20/21) — viewing a person's calendar:** an occurrence with **no responsible person is always shown** (unless its schedule is hidden), whatever its involved people; otherwise it is shown when its responsible person is shown **or** any involved person is shown. So viewing a single person shows everything except occurrences led by someone else in which that person is not involved. (With nobody shown, only occurrences without a responsible person remain.) Rule is per repetition, since each repetition has its own responsible person. Deleting a person removes them from all events. Person ⋯ menu: Colour…, Statistics… (not for external people), External (not one of us), Delete.
- **R-PPL-02** **[U]** **Reordering people:** each person row starts with a **grip** (six dots, shown on hover; always faintly visible on touch screens) — drag it up/down (drop line shows the target) or focus it and use the **arrow keys**. The order is data (undoable, unsaved) and sets the order of side-by-side lanes (R-EVT-10/13) — the order *as shown*, i.e. our people first, external people after them (`pIdx` in `laneLayout`). Activity types are reorderable the same way (R-TYP-05); the other lists are not yet.
- **R-PPL-03** **[U]** **External people** (`person.external:true`, only when set): someone from outside the household — a cleaner, a gardener, a babysitter — who does activities but isn't "one of us" **[U]**. Toggled in the person ⋯ menu (*External (not one of us)*, tick + explanatory tooltip, undoable data); the row shows a small external-link marker (`.extb`, labelled). Their rows are listed in the **External** sub-group of the People section (R-PPL-04). They stay normal people otherwise: own eye (visibility rule unchanged), the responsible list (adds *(external)*), lane order (after ours). **They are never offered as involved people** (the dialog's *With* chips list `ourPeople()` only) **[U]**. **Left out of the statistics** (person picker, *Everyone*, its table; `ourPeople()`); only external people → the "add people first" toast. Their activities are ordinary boxes; tick *In the background* (R-EVT-19) to draw one as a strip (R-EVT-17). `parseData` keeps the flag only when `true`; it travels with files, links, insert, transfer (a person created in the target keeps it) and anonymised exports.
- **R-PPL-04** **[U]** **External people are folded away** (they rarely matter): the People section lists our people first; external ones (R-PPL-03) follow under a collapsible sub-header **External (N)** (`.subh`, chevron, `aria-expanded`, ⓘ tip; when folded and some of them are hidden by their eye it adds *· N hidden*), **folded by default** (per-browser pref `secExt`; anything but `false` = folded) **[U]**. Unfolded, their rows (`.extg`) are the usual rows (grip, eye, icon, name, external marker, ⋯). No sub-header when there are no external people. The grip reorders **within its group** only (`gripFor(row, list, item, group)`): the person lands just before/after the group neighbour it passes, so the data positions of the other group are untouched **[C✓]**. Marking someone external just moves their row; the data order stays.
