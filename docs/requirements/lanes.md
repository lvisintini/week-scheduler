# Overlaps and side-by-side lanes

- **R-EVT-09** **[U]** Overlapping events that are drawn on top of each other (see R-EVT-10 for which ones): the overlapped range is drawn as **diagonal stripes mixing the overlapping colours** (45°, 7 px bands, 85 % of the highest opacity among the overlapping events), within that lane only. **[C✓]** Same-colour overlaps get a lightened stripe so they stay visible.
- **R-EVT-10** **[U]** **Side by side vs on top:** stripes mean "possible conflict", side by side means "in parallel, fine". Two overlapping occurrences in a day column **clash** — drawn **on top of each other with stripes** — when they **share a person** (responsible or involved, in either role; people *on call* don't count, R-EVT-16) **[U]** or either has **no responsible person** (even with involved people: they are busy, and nobody else is assigned, so it concerns everyone) **[U]**; otherwise they are **parallel** and go **side by side**. **Repetitions of one event follow the same rule** **[U]**: being the same event is no reason to clash (it used to be), so two overlapping repetitions performed by people who share nobody go side by side without stripes or ⚠ (R-REP-05); one rule, `clash()`, serves the lanes and the ⚠. Decided per occurrence (each repetition has its own responsible person) and from visible occurrences only (people filter, hidden schedules). Layout (`laneLayout`): each group of time-overlapping occurrences splits the column into as many lanes as it needs (non-overlapping events keep the full width) **[U]**; lanes are ordered by the People list as shown (responsible person; reorderable, R-PPL-02; external people after ours, R-PPL-04), then start time. Each occurrence gets a **range of lanes** (R-EVT-13, R-EVT-18). Lanes are computed, never stored: dragging sideways within a day changes nothing. Click/drag/resize/peek/right-click hit-testing uses the pointer's x (`laneMap`, `inLane`, `fxAt`), so side-by-side events open/move directly without a chooser. **[U]** Side by side **cannot be turned off**: the former Settings → View option *Show parallel events side by side* (`prefs.side`; off = everything overlapped with stripes) was removed because the app no longer makes sense without it, and the key is retired (never read again). When lanes get narrow, untick *Fit width*, raise the column width (R-GRID-07) and scroll sideways (help tip 6 says so). Background activities of external people (R-EVT-17) are not boxes and take no part in this at all; nested activities (R-NST-01) are taken out before the layout too and drawn inside their host.
- **R-EVT-13** **[U]** **Spanning lanes:** an occurrence that clashes with two (or more) occurrences that are parallel to each other **spans their lanes** instead of merging them: the parallel ones stay side by side and the spanning one overlaps each **independently**, striped only inside that lane (stripes are computed per group and lane; identical neighbouring lane segments are joined). Rule: parallel + overlapping ⇒ lane ranges disjoint; clashing + overlapping ⇒ ranges share a lane. Algorithm: occurrences that are not spanners are bundled (transitive clashes) and packed into lanes; each spanner takes the range from the leftmost to the rightmost lane of what it clashes with; the result is validated. **Lanes may be reordered** (all orders of up to 6 lanes, tried in order of closeness to the People order, People order first) so that the occurrences a spanner clashes with become neighbours **[U]**. The People order is always tried first and kept whenever it works, so lanes move only when that is needed to show the clashes; for that reason the former setting **Keep lanes in the order of the People list** (`prefs.sideFix`) was removed **[U]** and its key retired (a lane per person in People order is the Tools switch *Fixed lanes*, R-EVT-23). When no order works (or >6 lanes, or >60 occurrences in one group) that group **falls back to merging** its clashing occurrences into shared lanes with stripes — a deliberately distinct "something is wrong here" signal **[U]**. Groups in which nothing is parallel are one lane without any search. Hit-testing uses the range (`laneMap` = `[first, last, lanes]`); `data-lane` is `l/n` or `l0-l1/n`.
- **R-EVT-18** **[U]** **Reaching its people's lanes:** an occurrence also spans the **own lanes of its people** (responsible or involved; people only on call don't count, R-EVT-16) where nothing parallel is in the way, even when it clashes with nothing there. Example found by the user: Luis works 09:00–12:00 and 13:00–17:00, has lunch 12:00–13:00 with Ioanna, who works 10:00–16:00. The lunch clashes only with Ioanna's work, so it used to sit in her lane alone; now it spans Luis's lane (free between his two work blocks) and hers, striped only where it overlaps her work. **[C✓]** A person's *own lanes* are the lanes holding bundles led by that person alone (a mixed bundle, like the lunch with Ioanna's work, belongs to nobody; this keeps Luis's work from reaching for the lunch's lane). Applied after the spanning step to every order tried (`join` in `laneLayout`): the lane-order search takes the first order where every occurrence reaches its people's lanes, otherwise the valid order where the most do (ties: closest to the People order). It never makes a layout invalid: an occurrence grows only where it meets no parallel occurrence. **An occurrence without a responsible person concerns everyone** (R-EVT-10), so it spans **every lane of its group** **[U]** — always possible, since it clashes with everything it overlaps. Example found by the user: Luis and Ioanna both work 13:00–17:00 side by side (Luis may finish 15 minutes early), and the school pickup 16:45–18:00 (no responsible person, Olivia involved) used to sit in Ioanna's lane only; now it spans both, striped over her work and covering Luis's flexible end (a core covers margins, R-EVT-15).
- **R-EVT-23** **[U]** **Fixed lanes** (Tools switch *Fixed lanes, one per person*, `prefs.lanesFix`, default off, remembered per browser, R-UI-11): instead of the lanes worked out per group (`laneLayout`), **every one of our people whose eye is open has a lane of their own in every day column**, in the order of the People list as shown, and **a person's lane is never shared, even on a day they have nothing planned** **[U]** (`fixedLayout`). **External people** (R-PPL-03) get a lane, after ours, **only when they have at least one activity on the grid that is not in the background** (visible schedule, people and type filters; as performer or involved, not on call) — a gardener who only appears as a strip takes no room **[U]**; the check is week-wide, so every column has the same lanes. An occurrence is drawn **in the lane of each of its people** **[U]** — the performer and the involved people (not those on call, R-EVT-16; if none of those is visible, its visible on-call people) — with **runs of neighbouring lanes joined into one box** and **separated lanes drawn as separate boxes**, each with the full text, margins and resize handle (`segs`; `laneMap` holds `['m', segs, n]`, `inLane` accepts any of them, so clicking, dragging, peeking and right-clicking work on every box). **No responsible person** = every lane (it concerns everyone, R-EVT-10). Overlapping in a lane then always means sharing a person, so the stripes stay where the clashes are (one group per column, the usual per-lane stripe pass). A nested activity (R-NST-02) sits in its host's box that holds its own person's lane. Strips and their gutter are unchanged; with no visible person everything shares one lane. Lanes get narrow with many people: untick *Fit width* and widen the columns (R-GRID-07), as the tooltip says. Printing, the day view and Live View follow it.
