# Repetitions

- **R-REP-01** **[U]** An event can repeat on chosen days of the week. Each day has **its own times** (there are no event-level *general times* any more **[U]**; a newly ticked day starts with the times of the nearest ticked day above it, else below, else 09:00–10:00, performed by the owner, with the default involved people, fixed), its **own performer** (R-EVT-20) and its own involved people if it differs (R-EVT-21); all share colour/title/etc. Repetitions can be removed or retimed individually (dialog), and deleting the event deletes all.
- **R-REP-02** **[U]** **One day table, no modes** **[U]**: the dialog lists Mon–Sun; a ticked day's first time sits in its row, and its **+** (*Add another time on Monday*) adds **further times on that day** as lines below it (`.dr.dx`; starting where the day's last time ends — or with it after 23:00 —, an hour long, performed by the owner, With *Default*, fixed), each with its own start, end, performer, With, timing and a **✕**. The day's **⧉** makes **every other ticked day like this one** — all its times, performers, With lists, colours and timing, adding or removing their further times **[U]**. An event with several times on a day opens with those lines (in time order); unticking a day greys out all its lines (kept until saving, dropped on save). Moving a time to another day is done by dragging it on the grid. This **replaced the Quick and Advanced tabs** (Quick: one time per day; Advanced: a flat list of blocks with a day list, which locked Quick once a day had two) **[U]**.
- **R-REP-03** **[U]** Events may **run past midnight**: end up to `MAXEND = 2880` (midnight at the end of the *next* day). Overflow stops at the next day; **Sunday wraps into Monday**. Drawn as joined pieces with flat edges; labels `22:00–02:00 (Next day)` / `from Fri 22:00 · until 02:00`. Only the last piece has the resize handle.
- **R-REP-04** **[U]** End-time select labels: `…23:45`, `00:00 (Next day)`, `00:15 (Next day)` … `23:45 (Next day)`, `00:00 (Day after next)`; entries ≤ start are disabled. Compact displays (boxes, agenda, tooltips): plain midnight → `00:00`; any later end → `(Next day)` suffix, including the end of the next day (`00:00 (Next day)`). *Known inconsistency between select and compact notation — alignment proposed, not done* **[P]**.
- **R-REP-05** **[U]** **Overlapping repetitions are allowed but warned about** — **only when they clash** by the people rule of R-EVT-10 (a shared person, not on call, or either without a responsible person; phases have no people, so theirs always clash) **[U]**; `overlapSet(e, slots)`, and the dialog checks the values as they would be saved (performers, owner, With lists, *only on call*), updating on every change: ⚠ on the affected dialog rows + summary line; ⚠ icons on the grid pieces; a toast when a drag/resize *creates* new overlaps (with Undo).
- **R-REP-06** **[U]** **Drag/resize of a repeating event changes ALL repetitions by default** (live preview), **Shift** (also mid-drag) changes only the grabbed one. After an "all" drop a toast offers **Only this day** and **Undo**. Moves shift every repetition by the same minutes and days (mod 7); resizes change every end by the same amount; **minimum duration is 15 minutes — a repetition is never removed by resizing**. Dragging a resize handle into the next day's column extends past midnight. An event with at most one time per day blocks moving a single repetition onto a day that already has one; once a day has two times it is allowed (`isAdv`).
- **R-REP-07** **[U]** No confirmation dialog for repeat drags (an earlier confirm dialog was rejected).
