# Nested activities (small tasks done during another activity)

- **R-NST-01** **[U]** Some small tasks are done **along the way, during another activity of the same person**: e.g. *Collect milk* (15 min, three mornings a week, on the days the milk is delivered) is done at the end of the school run, but on Saturday, with no school run, it stands alone. It is a **real event** (own time, person, type, repetitions) with the per-event mark **`event.nest:true`** (stored only when set, like `oncall`; dialog: R-NST-05). **Rejected:** free-text "extras" on one occurrence (built and withdrawn): the task needs its own time and person and must be able to stand alone **[U]**. **Rule** (one helper for grid, agenda, drag and statistics): an occurrence piece of a marked event is **nested** when it has a responsible person P and its **core** (R-EVT-15) lies **entirely** within the windows (start…end, flexible margins included) of other occurrences that are **activities of P** — P responsible or involved, *not* on call — **any activity of that person**, not a chosen event **[U]**; several back to back may cover it together **[U]**. Hosts are never marked events themselves (no chains) and never background strips; strips themselves never nest. Its **host** is the occurrence covering the core's start. **Partly covered = not nested**: it is an ordinary occurrence that clashes as usual, so a slipped task stands out **[C✓]**. Hosts that are not drawn (hidden schedule, people filter) don't count on the grid; host and nested must be occurrences of the same week of the cycle (R-CYC-07). Nesting is **computed, never stored** (no link data).
- **R-NST-02** **[U]** **Drawn as an inset box** inside its host **at its own time and duration** **[U]**: a normal event box (rows, flags, clipping, 14 px minimum height like every box) with class `nest`, horizontally within the host's lane range **indented 14 px on the left** (also in a one-lane column), with a ring in the card colour, above the host's text and the stripes (`z-index:5`, drawn after the other boxes). Nested pieces are **taken out before `laneLayout`**: they take no lane, cause no stripes and never clash; the host keeps its lane. Spanning several hosts, it follows the lanes of the first one ("try it and see" **[U]**). **Hit-testing:** `laneMap` holds `['n', l, l1, n]`; `inLane` excludes the indent; `piecesAt` and `handlesAt` give a nested box priority over what is beneath, so it opens/drags/peeks directly and the host is reached beside it. Peek card, `aria-label` and day agenda say *During: School run* (*Durante:*); the host's peek card lists *Also during this: Collect milk 08:15–08:30*.
- **R-NST-03** **[C✓]** **Moving a host carries what is nested in it**: at the start of a move every nested occurrence hosted by a repetition of the grabbed event is recorded and follows **the shift of its own host repetition**, its week included (R-CYC-09) (so an all-repetitions move carries all of them, Shift carries only the grabbed day's, and the one standing alone on Saturday stays); an event with at most one time per day keeps it that way (a carried one that would land on a used day stays); locked ones never move. One undo step; *Only this day* puts the others back. Resizing a host carries nothing; dragging the nested one is a normal drag.
- **R-NST-04** **[U]** **Statistics:** the shared minutes **go to the nested activity** and the host loses them (so chores show up), computed from the visible schedules regardless of the people filter and the *involved* toggle; that time is **not** *Overlapping* (only two nested ones at the same time, or ordinary core on core, are).
- **R-NST-05** **[U]** **Dialog:** a checkbox in *How it fits in* (R-UI-07), under *Involved people are only on call*, **Can be done during the person's other activities** (*Se puede hacer durante otras actividades de la persona*, `#fNest`), with a tooltip explaining the drawing, the statistics and the carrying; events only. A help tip (help17) describes it.
